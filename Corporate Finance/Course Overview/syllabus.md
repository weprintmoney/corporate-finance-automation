### Course description

Every choice a corporation makes has financial implications. As a result, when defined broadly, every business decision fits under the rubric of corporate finance. In Corporate Finance with Professor Aswath Damodaran, students become familiar with this "big picture" of corporate finance while also learning the intricate theories and techniques that are crucial to maximizing shareholder value.

### Learning outcomes

By the end of this class, you should be able to:

- understand the theory and apply, in real world situations, the techniques that have been developed in corporate finance
- become familiar with the "big picture" of corporate finance so that you can understand how things fit together
- understand the reasoning behind major investing, financing and dividend decisions

### Course format and meetings

This is an online asynchronous course with optional synchronous sessions. The course consists of 36 online sessions, designed to capture what I do in my semester-long in-person corporate finance class. Students are encouraged to complete two sessions per week (as noted in the Course Outline). Check the Course Outline for the dates for the live Meetups (Zoom sessions), the 3 quizzes, and the final exam as these dates are fixed. The times and dates listed for this course are based on US Eastern Time (ET). Make sure to convert the time and dates given for your local time zone. A [time zone calculator](https://www.timeanddate.com/worldclock/converter.html) might be helpful. There is a lot of material for each session. You may not be able to cover all of it, which is fine. Each session is structured according to the following framework to help you prioritize:

- **Required activities:**
  - Review and download the Slide Sets
  - Watch lecture videos
  - Complete Self-Assessments
  - Review Readings
  - Complete Practice Problems
  - Complete required assignments to build toward the Cumulative Project
- **Optional activities** could include recommended reading materials from: Blogs, Application Webcasts, Datasets, and Spreadsheets & Tools

**Online meetups**

There will be six live online Meetups as indicated in the Course Outline. The Meetups are optional and planned to be recorded.

You can join by clicking on the Zoom tool on the top menu bar and then clicking "Join" for the Zoom session. We recommend testing Zoom to ensure your computer is ready for our synchronous online Meetups.

##### Quizzes

By the end of each module, you will need to complete one quiz that counts for 10% of your final grade.

Your lowest quiz score will be dropped. This means that if you take all three quizzes, your lowest quiz score will be replaced with your average score across the other two quizzes.

- You can never be worse off from taking all three quizzes. At worst, your total score will be unaffected, but at best, it will improve.

If you miss a quiz, even for a good reason, you will receive a zero for that quiz. Since your lowest quiz score will be dropped, the zero for the missed quiz could be dropped (and thus missing a quiz will not significantly affect your overall quiz score as long as you do well on the other two).

- You can only drop one quiz score, so missing 2 or more quizzes will result in zero(s) being used in your averages and grade calculations.

Quiz Policies:

- Quizzes are offered only on specific dates. Check the course outline.
- Do not post quiz questions in the discussions section of the course.
- The quizzes are NOT group work and should be completed INDIVIDUALLY.
- You cannot consult with, talk to, communicate with, or pass telepathic messages to anyone else in or out of this course.
