# Corporate Finance — Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I join an Online Meetup?

Navigate to the course site, click on the Zoom tab at the top of the course site, and click "Join." We encourage you to use your camera and microphone during the Online Meetups. This gives everyone an opportunity to get the most out of the experience and learn from each other. Please ensure you sign in with SSO and follow the instructions below to join a Zoom session:

- If you are asked to confirm an NYU email address, please check the email account you registered for the course with to confirm your email address. As a non-credit certificate student, you do not have access to an NYU email address. Therefore, please check your personal email address to confirm your email address. If you do not see the confirmation in your inbox, please check your spam folder.
- We recommend that you test your device before the live session to ensure that you are ready when the session begins. Please visit the Zoom device-test link to test your device (open the link in a new tab in your browser).
- Attendance at these sessions is optional.

## I cannot attend an Online Meetup. Will I be able to watch it at a later time? Will I lose points for not attending the online meetup?

You are not required to join the Online Meetups. All of the online meetups are recorded and can be found on the course site within 24 hours after the online meetup has concluded.

To view the recordings, please visit the Zoom tab on the top of the course site. Then select "Cloud Recordings."

Once you click on the recording you would like to view, please follow the below instructions to access the recording. You need to ensure you are signing in with SSO in the Zoom tab of the course site. Instead of "joining" the class, you will go back to the cloud recording and press play on the available recording.

The Online Meetups are optional, and we encourage your participation. However, your attendance will not impact your grade.

## I missed a quiz. Can I reschedule?

There are no opportunities to reschedule a missed quiz. Please keep in mind that your lowest quiz score is dropped. Be sure to note the quiz dates, which can be found in the Syllabus and Course Outline under the Content tab. It is best to review the quiz dates in the course outline and the grading structure in the Syllabus. The Syllabus and Course Outline can be found in the "Syllabus" tab on the left side of the course site.

## When will I get CapIQ access and how long will I have access to CapIQ?

Your Capital IQ login will be created about 4–6 weeks after the course begins. You will receive an email from Capital IQ with information on how to log in. Please check your spam folder if you do not see the email in your inbox. Once you have access, please view the training video which is available in the course site on the Capital IQ page.

Your Capital IQ access will be terminated when the course concludes. The date the course concludes can be found in the course outline in the course site.

## How long after the course ends will I continue to have access to the course site, lessons, and resources?

You will have access to the course site (including all messages, resources, etc.) until up to 30 days after the course concludes. Access to the course materials after the course finish date is subject to change without notification.

## How will I know I have passed the course and earned a certificate?

In order to pass the course and earn a certificate, you need to score a 55% or higher. Follow the steps below to access your final course grade:

1. Navigate to the Grades tab on the top of the course site.
2. Your grade will be listed in the Final Grades column.
3. The grades will be posted in the gradebook about 3–4 weeks after the final day of the course.

The course is Pass/Fail — if you have a 55% or above, you have successfully completed the course and will receive a digital certificate within 4–6 weeks. If you see a **P**, you have passed the course and earned the certificate. If you see an **H** ("Honors"), you have passed the course with a score above 90%.

## When will I receive my certificate?

You will receive a digital certificate within 4–6 weeks after the final grades have been posted in the Gradebook on the course site. You will receive an email which includes a link to your badge and digital certificate.

## What if I successfully passed the course but did not receive my digital certificate?

If you did not receive an email, please contact certificatesupport@stern.nyu.edu and they will resend your badge and certificate.

## What will my certificate say on it?

Your certificate will say the following: "This [certificate] is to certify that *Student Name* has successfully completed all requirements of The NYU Stern Certificate In Corporate Finance."

Your badge will simply state "Certificate in Corporate Finance." This badge can be shared on social media platforms.

## I would love to stay connected with the Certificate Community. Are there any opportunities to do so?

Once you have successfully completed the course, you will be invited to join the LinkedIn Alumni Group where you can continue to network with your classmates and meet those who completed the course before you. You will also qualify for a 15% discount on future online certificate courses.

## How do I reach out for help?

- For help regarding technical difficulties or course site questions, please reach out to the Course Support Team: certificatesupport@stern.nyu.edu.
- For content-based questions or questions regarding an assignment, please reach out to the instructor or TF. You can find the contact information in the Syllabus.
