# Lesson 6 — Equity Risk Premiums

## Overview

To determine what you would demand for investing in the equity in a business, you need to know what the price of risk is in the equity market. That price of risk is captured with an equity risk premium, but measuring it is not easy. This lesson looks at three different ways to estimate the equity risk premium — surveying investors, looking at the historical premium earned by equities over something risk free, and a forward-looking (implied) premium — and compares their pluses and minuses.

**Learning objectives:**
- Understand what an equity risk premium measures and why it matters
- Estimate equity risk premiums using three approaches: surveys, historical data, and implied (forward-looking) premiums
- Compare the advantages and limitations of each estimation approach
