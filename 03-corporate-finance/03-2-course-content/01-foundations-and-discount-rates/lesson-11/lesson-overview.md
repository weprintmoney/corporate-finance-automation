# Lesson 11 — The "Right" Beta

## Overview

The bottom-up beta approach can yield better estimates of beta for a company, but it needs to be refined and adapted when the company is a private business whose owners may not be diversified. This lesson focuses on estimating the beta for a private business, with undiversified or partially diversified owners, introducing the concept of total beta as the appropriate risk measure in those cases.

**Learning objectives:**
- Distinguish between market beta (for diversified investors) and total beta (for undiversified owners)
- Estimate total beta using the ratio of total risk to market risk
- Apply the right beta concept to private businesses, closely held firms, and owner-operated companies
