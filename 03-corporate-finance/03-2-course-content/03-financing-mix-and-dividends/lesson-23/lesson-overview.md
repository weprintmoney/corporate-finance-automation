# Lesson 23 — The Right Financing

## Overview

If you can match your financing cash flows to the cash flows on your operations, you will reduce default risk and increase value. Consequently, the right type of debt for your firm will depend on what types of investments/assets it owns. By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

**Learning objectives:**
- Determine the maturity, the currency, convertibility and other features of your debt.
