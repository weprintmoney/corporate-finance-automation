# Lesson 24 — Trends and Measures

## Overview

Dividend policy, more than any other aspect of corporate finance, has distinctive and long-standing patterns that companies seem unwilling to deviate from. Before you look at a specific company's dividend policy, it is good to get a sense of these market-wide patterns. By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

**Learning objectives:**
- Understand how companies have approached setting and changing dividends historically and why more and more companies have shifted in the last few decades toward stock buybacks (as a way of returning cash).
