# Lesson 25 — The Trade Off

## Overview

There is a trade off, since paying dividends can have positive effects (by signaling predictable earnings to markets and rewarding the investors who bought your stock because you pay dividends) and negative effects (investors may have to pay more taxes and you are signaling that you cannot find projects that earn good returns)

**Learning objectives:**
- A Brief History of Dividend Tax Rates
- Dividends and Taxes: An Analysis of the Bush Tax Plan (2003)
- Tax Policy and the Dividend Clientele Effect (2003)
- When a dividend signals weakness (Bloomberg)
