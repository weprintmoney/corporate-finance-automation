# Lesson 20 — The Cost of Capital: Wrap Up

## Overview

The cost of capital approach to finding the optimal mix of debt and equity is versatile but will need adaptation when used at companies whose operating earnings are moved by macroeconomic forces out of their control and for private businesses. By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

**Learning objectives:**
- Compute the optimal debt ratio for commodity/cyclical companies, for family group companies and privately owned businesses.

## Project Questions

- If your company is a commodity or cyclical company, how would you adjust your optimal debt ratio to reflect earnings volatility?
- If your company is part of a family group, what other considerations might come into play in determining your optimal debt ratio?
- If your company is privately owned, how would your optimal debt ratio be computed differently?
