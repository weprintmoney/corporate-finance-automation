# Lesson 22 — Moving to the Optimal

## Overview

When a firm has chosen to fund itself with a mix of debt and equity that is not optimal for it, it has choices on whether it should move to the optimal mix, and if so, both how quickly and with what actions (taking projects or recapitalizing itself). By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

**Learning objectives:**
- Develop a customized pathway for getting a firm from its actual to its optimal debt ratio.

## Project Questions

- Is your firm under, over or correctly levered?
- If under levered, is it under threat of a hostile acquisition? If over levered, how imminent is the bankruptcy threat?
- How good are the potential projects that the company can invest in?
- What is the best path that you see for your firm to get from its actual to its optimal debt ratio?
