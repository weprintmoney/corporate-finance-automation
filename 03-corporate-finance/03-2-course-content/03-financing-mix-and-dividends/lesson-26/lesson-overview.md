# Lesson 26 — Assessments

## Overview

Whether it be in dividends or buybacks, businesses are returning cash to investors, but to make an assessment of whether they are returning enough, too much or too little, we need to compare the actual cash returned to the cash that could have been returned during a period. By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

**Learning objectives:**
- Estimate potential dividends, i.e., the cash flow left over after all operating, financing and investment needs have been met, and compare to actual cash returned (dividends and buybacks).

## Project Questions

- How much has your company returned to its stockholders, in the form of dividends and stock buybacks in the last five years?
- How much cash could your company have returned in the last five years? (Estimate FCFE)
- If there is a difference, where is it showing up (cash balances, debt ratios)?
