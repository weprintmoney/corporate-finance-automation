# Lesson 7 — Country Risk Premiums

## Overview

As companies and investors globalize, we face the challenge of estimating equity risk premiums in foreign equity markets — many of which may be young, transitioning, or have very different risk characteristics than the domestic market. To value a company that gets its revenues and value from many countries, we need to estimate a country risk premium. This lesson starts with measures of country risk (default or political) and examines ways to convert those country risk measures into equity risk premiums.

**Learning objectives:**
- Identify appropriate measures of country risk, including sovereign default risk and political risk
- Convert country risk measures into equity risk premiums
- Estimate a company's exposure to country risk based on its geographic revenue mix
