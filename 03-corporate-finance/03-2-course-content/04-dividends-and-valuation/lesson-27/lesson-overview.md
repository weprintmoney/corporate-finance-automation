# Lesson 27 — Action and Follow Up

## Overview

A company that is returning too little cash to its shareholders will build up a cash balance and one that is returning too much cash will deplete existing cash balances. Any imbalance between actual cash returned and potential dividends will eventually give rise to a problem but when and in what form will depend on how much investors trust a company. By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

**Learning objectives:**
- Determine the optimal course of action for a company, in terms of cash return, given its actual and potential dividends as well as answer the question of when a cash balance for a company has become "too big".

## Project Questions

- If your firm has returned less cash than it can over time and has a big cash balance, do you trust the firm with your cash?
- If your firm has returned more cash than it can over time, should it scale back cash return?
