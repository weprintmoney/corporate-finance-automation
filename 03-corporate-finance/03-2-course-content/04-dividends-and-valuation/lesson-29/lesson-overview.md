# Lesson 29 — First Steps

## Overview

When assessing what a company is worth, you can either value the company (by looking at its cash flows, growth and risk) or price the company (by looking at what investors are paying for similar companies). The two can yield different values and which one you use will depend on why you are making the assessment. By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

**Learning objectives:**
- Understand the difference between valuing and pricing a business and also what the fundamental differences are in how you value just the equity in a business or the entire business.
