# Lesson 35 — Relative Valuation

## Overview

The price of a company reflects what investors will be willing to pay for the company today, given its characteristics (growth, risk and cash flow) and what investors are paying for similar companies in the marketplace. By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

**Learning objectives:**
- Price a company, using a multiple or multiples and comparable firms.

## Project Questions

- What multiples of earnings, book value and revenue does your company trade at?
- Which of these multiples is the best one to price your company on?
- What are the "comparable" firms that you would use in pricing your company?
- Based upon the multiple and comparables, what price would you attach to your company?
