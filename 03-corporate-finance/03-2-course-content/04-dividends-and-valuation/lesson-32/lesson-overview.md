# Lesson 32 — Terminal Value

## Overview

In most intrinsic valuations, the single largest cash flow determining value is the terminal value and that terminal value, left unchecked or estimated loosely, can cause valuations to implode. Constrained appropriately, terminal value will become a less critical driver of a firm's value. By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

**Learning objectives:**
- Develop a set of simple rules for keeping intrinsic value in check, starting with a cap on the growth rate (forever) and extending into making sure that you are making consistent assumptions about reinvestment and risk.

## Project Questions

- When (in what year) will your firm be a mature firm (where you can estimated terminal value)?
- What will your firm's growth rate be in perpetuity after that point in time?
- What return on and cost of capital would you give your firm, when it becomes a mature firm?
