# Lesson 33 — To Value Per Share

## Overview

While it is important that you estimate cash flows, growth and discount rates to get to an value for the operating assets, there are loose ends to tie up, if you want to get from that value to the value of equity, including what debt to net out, how to deal with cross holdings and management options. By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

**Learning objectives:**
- Develop a structured process for bringing in what you have not counted already in your valuation (minority cross holdings, cash, vacant assets) and what will reduce the value of your common stockholders (debt, minority interests from consolidations, equity options), to get to the value of equity.

## Project Questions

- How much cash does your firm have? Would you attach a discount or premium to it?
- Does your firm have minority cross holdings? What is their value?
- Does your firm have majority cross holdings? How have you incorporated their value?
- Have managers in the firm been compensated with equity options or restricted stock? How does that affect your value per share?
