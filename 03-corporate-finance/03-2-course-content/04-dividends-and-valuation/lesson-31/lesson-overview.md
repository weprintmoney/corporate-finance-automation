# Lesson 31 — Growth

## Overview

The intrinsic value of a company is a function of its growth, but going for more growth creates a trade off: lower cash flow today for higher cash flows in the future. Put simply, growth can add value for a firm, do nothing for value or even destroy value. By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

**Learning objectives:**
- Determine the free cash flow for a firm and how it is affected by growth expectations. Follow up by developing a simple rule for gauging the value of growth.

## Project Questions

- What is the historical growth rate in your company's revenues? EBIT? Net income?
- What are analysts or managers projecting as an expected growth rate in the future?
- Assuming that your company continues to earn its current ROIC/ROE and maintaining its existing reinvestment rate, what would the expected growth rate be in earnings?
- If margins are changing, what are your estimates of revenue growth, margins and reinvestment for the future?
