# Lesson 28 — The End Game

## Overview

Investors pick companies often based upon their dividend policies, gravitating towards companies that have policies that they like. Changing dividend policy or initiating dividends is therefore tricky, even if the action makes sense, since there will be investors who will not like the change (and sell their shares). By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

**Learning objectives:**
- Look at ways in which companies can change dividend policy while minimizing the effect on long-standing investor clienteles.
