# Lesson 19 — The Cost of Capital: Follow Up

## Overview

Changing your cost of capital, by altering your mix of debt and equity, can be a consequential decision, not just for value but for risk. It is important that you look at not only the possibility of things going wrong in your business but different ways in which you can change your mix. By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

**Learning objectives:**
- Calculate the change in value, when your cost of capital changes, and examine how sensitive your "optimal" mix of debt and equity is to both macro and micro risks.

## Project Questions

- If you move to the optimal debt ratio (given the cost of capital) approach, what consequences do you see for the value of your firm and the value of equity in the firm?
- Bringing in reasonable constraints into the cost of capital approach, what would your recommended debt ratio be for this firm?
