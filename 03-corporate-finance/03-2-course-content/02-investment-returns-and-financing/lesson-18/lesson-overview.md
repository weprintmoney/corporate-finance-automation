# Lesson 18 — The Cost of Capital: Approach

## Overview

The mix of debt and equity that you use as a firm can change your cost of capital, and by doing so, your value as a business. If your objective in corporate finance is to maximize value, the optimal mix of debt and equity is the one that minimizes your cost of capital. By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

**Learning objectives:**
- Estimate the cost of capital, at different debt ratio, by estimating the costs of equity and debt at that debt ratio.
