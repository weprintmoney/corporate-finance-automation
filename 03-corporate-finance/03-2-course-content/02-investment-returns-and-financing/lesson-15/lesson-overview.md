# Lesson 15 — Time Weighting Cash Flows

## Overview

Since you invest cash (not book value) in investments and you cannot spend earnings (but can spend cash), it is the magnitude and timing of cash flows on an investment that determine its value. By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

**Learning objectives:**
- Estimate cash flows on an investment or a firm, and use them to measure investment quality, from time-based (payback) to dollar-based or absolute values (Net Present Value) to percentage returns (Internal Rate of Returns).
