- "The Board believes that, given the Company's present circumstances, having a combined Chair..." (same file, Board leadership structure). Shareholders rejected an independent-chair proposal on 2026-05-21, 186.3M for, 529.4M against (`hd-8-k-2026-05-26-items-503-507-901.md`, Proposal 9).

(`hd-def14a-2026-04-07.md`, Compensation discussion, Performance shares)

For fiscal 2026 the sales threshold rose from 90% to 95% of target. (same file, Annual cash incentive)

## Hurdle and returns

- No hurdle rate is disclosed. ROIC is the stated return measure: "We believe ROIC is meaningful for management, investors and ratings agencies because it measures how effectively we deploy our capital base." ROIC 25.7% in fiscal 2025, 31.3% in fiscal 2024, 36.7% in fiscal 2023. (`hd-10-k-2026-03-18.md`, Non-GAAP measures)
- "The decrease in ROIC was primarily driven by higher average equity due to our ongoing pause in share repurchases and higher average long-term debt largely due to the financing of the SRS acquisition." (same, MD&A executive summary)
