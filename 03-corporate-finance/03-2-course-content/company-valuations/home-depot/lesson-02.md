- **Who sits on the board, whose interests do they serve, where does power lie?**

  Twelve directors, eleven independent; Edward Decker is Chair, President and CEO; Gregory Brenneman (director since 2000) is Lead Director (`governance.board`). No change-in-control agreements, no poison pill, 15% of holders can call a special meeting (`governance.change_of_control_payouts`, `share_classes`). Power lies with management and a board that has kept the combined chair: an independent-chair proposal got 186.3M votes for, 529.4M against on 2026-05-21 (`facts: independent-chair-vote-for`). Holders are diversified index funds (Vanguard 10.0%, BlackRock 7.1%; insiders 0.08%), who rarely press capital-allocation fights. Governance strength: good on paper (independence, majority voting), but the shareholders who could discipline management are passive, so management's preferences set capital allocation. Pay: MIP weights sales 50% (up from 40%), operating profit 30%; performance shares are half three-year ROIC on book equity plus long-term debt, half operating profit (`facts: mip-sales-weight-fy2025`, `psu-roic-weight`).
