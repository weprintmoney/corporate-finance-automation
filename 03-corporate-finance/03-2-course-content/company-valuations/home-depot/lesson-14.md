## Cumulative Project — Questions for This Lesson

- **What return on capital did your firm generate last year?**

  20.41% (after-tax EBIT $15,667.5M ÷ invested capital $76,774M, with leases in capital; model-output.md section 8). The company's own ROIC, on a narrower capital base, was 25.7% (`facts: roic-company-fy2025`).

- **Is there a trend?**

  Down: 32.84% (fiscal 2023), 24.00% (2024), 20.41% (2025) on the course measure; company measure 36.7% → 31.3% → 25.7% → 25.4% trailing at Q1 fiscal 2026 (`facts: roic-company-*`). Invested capital rose $27,247M, about $23,054M of it acquisitions and the rest equity retained during the buyback pause, while operating income fell (`facts`).

- **How do returns compare with costs?**

  ROIC 20.41% vs cost of capital 9.83%: +10.58 points, EVA $8,119M (section 8). ROE 102% is an artifact of book equity shrunk by buybacks. Returns still exceed the hurdle by a wide margin; the trend, not the level, is the signal.

- **Will future projects look like past ones?**

  No. The marginal project is now a distribution acquisition. SRS plus GMS earned operating income of $316M on $12,717M of sales in fiscal 2025, a 2.5% margin after acquisition amortization (5.6% before its $398M of amortization, `facts`), against $20,574M on $151,966M (13.5%) in the stores (`facts: srs-gms-*`, `primary-operating-income-fy2025`). Goodwill is 29.1% of invested capital (section 8). The new capital earns much less than the old.
