- **How does your company dividend and cash return policy measure up against other companies in the sector?**
  JPMorgan returned 85.7% of 2025 net income; Bank of America 96.7%, Wells Fargo 107.5%, Citigroup 130.2%, Goldman 102.7%, PNC 56.8%, U.S. Bancorp 48.3% (bank-equity-output.md section F; `peers`). ## Notes

JPMorgan pays out less than its money-center peers despite the highest ROE among them: it is choosing to keep more cushion, not being forced to.
