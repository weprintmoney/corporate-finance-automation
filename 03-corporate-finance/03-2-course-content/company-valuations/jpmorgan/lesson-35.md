## Cumulative Project — Questions for This Lesson

- **Based upon the multiple and comparables, what price would you attach to your company?**
  Price-to-book is the multiple (lesson 35, banks). The 2.56x model figure (model-output.md section 7) compares with a peer median of 1.54x, but that comparison ignores ROE. Across seven peers P/B = −0.68 + 20.88 × ROE (R² 0.74); at JPMorgan's 16.3% ROE the line gives 2.72x, against 2.56x actual (bank-equity-output.md section F). That is about $349 a share on $128.38 of book, 7% above $327.33. Within the noise of a seven-bank regression on rough peer book values, JPMorgan is priced in line with its ROE.

## Notes

> ⚠️ **Discretionary Decision: peer set and multiple**
> **Chose:** BAC, C, WFC, GS, MS, PNC, USB on price-to-book, regressed on ROE. **Why:** the US banks large enough to be compared; P/B is the multiple the course uses for banks. **Alternative:** money-center peers only (BAC, C, WFC) would give a much lower median (about 1.3x) and call JPMorgan expensive; that is the wrong comparison because their ROEs are 7–13%. **Confidence:** medium; peer equity mixes total and common book (tool limitation).
