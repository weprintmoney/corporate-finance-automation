# JPMorgan Chase & Co. (JPM)

JPMorgan Chase & Co. is the largest US bank holding company (Delaware; NYSE: JPM; SEC CIK 19617). Type `public`, reporting in USD, fiscal year ending December 31. Base year 2025, with the 1Q26 10-Q for the latest capital and share data. Analysis date 2026-06-30. Quick mode, blind: one scored run in the 2026-06-30 backtest (`../backtests/2026-06-30/protocol.md`), where JPM is the bank and is scored separately. Because it is a bank, debt is treated as raw material, not financing (lessons 11, 20, 26). The leverage and debt-design signals are read from regulatory capital, and value against price is read from price-to-book against ROE. `meta.omitted` in `inputs.yaml` lists every firm-level field and model section that does not apply.

## Files

- `read.md` — the deliverable: situation, signals, unstated goals, what to watch.
- `inputs.yaml` — the ledger; every number with its source and date.
- `model-output.md` — generated by `tools/model.py`. Section 1 (cost of equity), section 7 (multiples) and section 8 (ROE against cost of equity) are used. Sections 2–4 print but do not apply.
- `bank-equity.py` / `bank-equity-output.md` — generated bank calculations: regulatory capital against requirements, bank FCFE against cash returned, justified price-to-book, peer P/B against ROE. They come from the ledger and the model output, because model.py has no bank section.
- `alternatives/` — ledger copies with one choice changed (beta ×3, country ERP) and their model outputs (SKILL rule 14).
- `lesson-01.md` … `lesson-36.md` — the 38 signal questions, in the 21 lessons that have them.
- `sources/` — filings, peer data, market data, stated positions, `published.yaml`, `README.md`.
- No `exhibits/` (see `meta.omitted`) and no `walkthrough.md` (optional for this backtest).
