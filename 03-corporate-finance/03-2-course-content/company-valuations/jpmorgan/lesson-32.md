## Cumulative Project — Questions for This Lesson

- **What return on and cost of capital would you give your firm, when it becomes a mature firm?**
  It already is one. Sustained ROE 16–17% against a cost of equity of 8.27% (beta 0.85), with growth of 3% (`market.terminal_growth`). A bank's ROE should fade toward its cost of equity in the long run; JPMorgan's has stayed 8.7–10.7 points above it in every period since 2023 (lesson 14), so the read tests 14% as the fade case (bank-equity-output.md section E).

## Notes

> ⚠️ **Discretionary Decision: stable growth and ROE**
> **Chose:** g = 3%, sustained ROE 17% (base) and 14% (fade). **Why:** 3% sits between the 2.3% fundamental growth and the 4.18% risk-free cap; 17% is the 2023–25 average. **Alternative:** g = 4% → justified P/B 3.04x instead of 2.66x at ROE 17%; ROE 14% → 2.09x (bank-equity-output.md section E). **Confidence:** low. The justified multiple is very sensitive near the cost of equity, which is why the read leans on the implied-ROE form.
