# JPMorgan Chase & Co. (JPM) — Lesson 04: Define and Measure Risk

### Lesson 04 — Define and Measure Risk

Quick mode: only the question-bank entries tagged with a signal. Source lesson: [../../01-foundations-and-discount-rates/lesson-04/lesson-overview.md](../../01-foundations-and-discount-rates/lesson-04/lesson-overview.md).

## Module 1 Step — Questions for This Lesson

- **How many classes of shares exist and what are the voting rights of each?**
  One class of voting common stock; the preferred series are non-cumulative and non-voting except as the law requires (`governance.share_classes`).

- **Who are the largest shareholders?**
  The Vanguard Group 9.86% and BlackRock 7.15% (proxy 5% holders table, as of 2025-12-31). Dimon owns about 0.23% (6,266,647 shares).

- **Is the marginal investor an individual or an institution?**
  An institution; the two largest are index managers (`governance.marginal_investor`).

- **Is that marginal investor diversified?**
  Yes. That is why the cost of equity uses a market (CAPM) beta rather than total risk.

## Notes

The marginal investor is diversified; market beta is the right risk measure (lessons 8–11).
