# JPMorgan Chase & Co. (JPM) — Lesson 14: Earnings and Cash Flows

### Lesson 14 — Earnings and Cash Flows

Quick mode: only the question-bank entries tagged with a signal. Source lesson: [../../02-investment-returns-and-financing/lesson-14/lesson-overview.md](../../02-investment-returns-and-financing/lesson-14/lesson-overview.md).

## Cumulative Project — Questions for This Lesson

- **What return on capital did your firm generate last year?**
  Return on equity is the measure for a bank. 2025: 17% on average common equity as reported (facts `roe-reported-2025`), 16.3% on year-end common equity (model-output.md section 8), ROTCE 20%. The ROIC line in section 8 (15.9%) is pre-tax income taxed at 25% over equity, a near-duplicate of ROE.

- **If you have returns on capital over time for your firm, is there a trend in those returns?**
  Flat to up: 17% (2023), 18% (2024), 17% (2025), 19% annualized in 1Q26 (bank-equity-output.md section C).

- **How do the returns on capital (equity) compare to the costs of capital (equity)?**
  ROE exceeds the 8.27% cost of equity by 8.7 to 10.7 points every period (section C); 8.0 points on year-end equity (model-output.md section 8). Even at the 9.00% market-beta cost of equity the spread is 8 points. Every segment clears it: 1Q26 ROE CCB 32%, CIB 21%, AWM 44%.

- **Are the projects in the future likely to look like the projects in the past? Why or why not?**
  Mostly. 2023–25 returns were helped by high rates on a deposit base that cost little and by First Republic purchase accretion; management's 2026 net interest income outlook of ~$103,000M (facts `nii-2026-guidance`) is above 2025's $95,443M, so it does not expect a reversal this year. Expenses are guided up to ~$105,000M from $95,640M. New money goes to card (Apple Card, ~$30,000M of RWA), CIB lending and international consumer; their returns will be lower than the deposit franchise's.

## Notes

> ⚠️ **Discretionary Decision: ROE measure**
> **Chose:** reported ROE on average common equity (17%) for the trend, net income to common ÷ year-end common equity (16.3%) for the model. **Why:** the filing's own number for comparability, the ledger's for traceability. **Alternative:** ROTCE 20% (excludes $54,082M goodwill) → spread to cost of equity 11.7 points. **Confidence:** high; the sign and size of the spread do not depend on it.
