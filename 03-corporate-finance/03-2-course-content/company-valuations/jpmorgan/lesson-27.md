## Cumulative Project — Questions for This Lesson

- **If your firm has returned less cash than it can over time and has a big cash balance, do you trust the firm with your cash?**
  That was 2024: CET1 built to 15.7%. On the record, yes: ROE has stayed 17–19% while the cushion grew, so retained capital was not wasted. The reason given at the time was regulatory uncertainty (Basel III proposal of 2023).

- **If your firm has returned more cash than it can over time, should it scale back cash return?**
  Not yet. Returning $47,700M against $29,894M of FCFE in 2025 drew down excess that is still $54,098M above today's requirement. It should scale back if the 2028 requirement (12.5%, or ~13.0% under the re-proposals) starts to bind: at the 1Q26 pace of drawdown (Standardized CET1 down 0.3 points in the quarter) CET1 would reach ~13% in about four to five quarters, so the pace has to slow by mid-2027 unless requirements fall.
