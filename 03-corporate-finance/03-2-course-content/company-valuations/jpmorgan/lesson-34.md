## Cumulative Project — Questions for This Lesson

- **What is the status quo value of your firm?**
  Not as a DCF (the firm DCF does not apply; `forecast.years` omitted). On the bank method, justified price-to-book at ROE 17%, cost of equity 8.27%, g 3% is 2.66x book, or $341 a share on $128.38 of book (bank-equity-output.md section E); the market paid 2.55x ($327.33). Put the other way, the price implies a sustained ROE of 16.4% at the base cost of equity, 15.4% at 7.85%, 18.3% at 9.00%.

- **What is the value of your firm with these changes made?**
  The one change on the table is distributing the excess CET1. At a price near justified value, buying back stock with the $54,098M excess neither creates nor destroys much value; it raises ROE (less equity) and per-share growth but not value per share. The value change is small.

- **What is the likelihood that this change will happen?**
  High for gradual distribution (a $50,000M authorization effective 2026-07-01 and a 10% dividend increase to $1.65 announced 2026-06-24, S-15), low for a step-change: the 2028 GSIB surcharge and the Basel re-proposal make management hold the cushion. The control question that could change policy is CEO succession: the board says 'medium-term' (S-2) and elevated two Co-Presidents on 2026-06-25 (S-3).
