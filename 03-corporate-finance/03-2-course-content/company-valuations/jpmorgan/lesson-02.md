# JPMorgan Chase & Co. (JPM) — Lesson 02: The Objective: Utopia and Let Down

### Lesson 02 — The Objective: Utopia and Let Down

Quick mode: only the question-bank entries tagged with a signal. Source lesson: [../../01-foundations-and-discount-rates/lesson-02/lesson-overview.md](../../01-foundations-and-discount-rates/lesson-02/lesson-overview.md).

## Module 1 Step — Questions for This Lesson

- **Who sits on the board of directors?**
  Eleven nominees, ten independent (`governance.board`): James Dimon (Chairman and CEO), Stephen Burke (Lead Independent Director since 2021, chairs the Compensation & Management Development Committee), Linda Bammann (Risk chair, a former JPMorgan risk executive retired 2005), Michele Buck, Alicia Boler Davis, Alex Gorsky, Mellody Hobson, Phebe Novakovic, Virginia Rometty, Brad Smith, Mark Weinberger. Todd Combs resigned 2025-12-07, 'not the result of any disagreement' (sources/filings/jpm-8-k-2025-12-08-items-502.md).

- **Whose interests are they most likely to serve?**
  Management's, as framed by Dimon, checked by regulators more than by shareholders. The CEO is also chair; the lead director has served since 2004 with Dimon (both came from Bank One). The board's own priority list leads with succession (sources/statements/jpm-stated-positions.md S-1, S-2).

- **Where does power lie, and what measure of governance strength supports that?**
  With the CEO and, for capital, with the Federal Reserve. No holder has more than 9.86% and all directors and officers together own under 1% (`governance.controlling_holders`, `governance.marginal_investor`). The binding constraint on payout is the regulatory requirement (11.5% CET1, facts `cet1-requirement-current`), set by the Fed's stress test. Shareholder engagement covered about 37% of the stock in 2025 (facts `shareholders-engaged-pct`). The board recommends against an independent-chair proposal again in 2026 (proxy, proposal 5).

## Notes

Control signal: diffuse institutional ownership, combined chair/CEO, regulator as the effective second principal. Succession is the live control question.
