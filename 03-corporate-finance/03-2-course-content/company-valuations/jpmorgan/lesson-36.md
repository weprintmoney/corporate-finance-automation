- **What is the narrative that you would use to describe your company, as its run today?**
  The most profitable large US bank, run by a 70-year-old CEO whose board says the succession is 'medium-term', holding more capital than today's rules require but not much more than 2028's will, and handing back what its balance sheet growth does not need. The market prices it at 2.55x book, about what a 16–17% sustained ROE is worth at an 8.3% cost of equity. The open questions are when the CEO changes and where the regulators set the 2028 requirement.
