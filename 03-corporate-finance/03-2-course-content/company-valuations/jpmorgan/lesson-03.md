# JPMorgan Chase & Co. (JPM) — Lesson 03: The Objective: Reality and Reaction

### Lesson 03 — The Objective: Reality and Reaction

Quick mode: only the question-bank entries tagged with a signal. Source lesson: [../../01-foundations-and-discount-rates/lesson-03/lesson-overview.md](../../01-foundations-and-discount-rates/lesson-03/lesson-overview.md).

## Module 1 Step — Questions for This Lesson

- **If the firm states goals, what is it targeting (growth, profitability, stock price, value)?**
  A 'fortress balance sheet' that 'focuses on risk-adjusted returns, strong capital and robust liquidity' (`governance.objectives`; S-8), with capital objectives to invest through cycles, 'retain flexibility to take advantage of future investment opportunities' and 'meet capital distribution objectives' (S-5). Pay targets ROTCE: the CEO's PSUs and the June 2026 retention awards both vest on ROTCE (S-3, S-4). No numeric ROTCE target, CET1 target or price goal appears in the filings (S-7, S-9).

- **If it states none, what does its behaviour say the primary focus is?**
  Behaviour says return on tangible equity above all, with the capital cushion run down slowly: CET1 fell from 15.7% (2024) to 14.3% (1Q26) while 85.7% of 2025 net income went back to shareholders (bank-equity-output.md sections A and B).

## Notes

Stated objective is safety plus returns; revealed objective weights returns (ROTCE in pay) and a shrinking but still large cushion.
