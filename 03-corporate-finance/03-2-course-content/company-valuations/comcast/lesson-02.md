## Module 1 Step — Questions for This Lesson

- **Who sits on the board of directors?**
  Eleven directors (`governance.board`): Brian L. Roberts (Chairman and Co-CEO), Michael J. Cavanagh (Co-CEO), and nine independents led by Edward D. Breen as Lead Independent Director (Bacon, Baltimore, Bell, Brady, Honickman, Lucas, Nakahara, Smith).

- **Whose interests are they most likely to serve?**
  Roberts'. The independents are elected annually, but Roberts' Class B stock carries a non-dilutable 33 1/3% of the vote (fact `roberts-class-b-vote`) plus a separate veto over mergers, a sale of substantially all assets and vote-requiring share issuances (`governance.share_classes`). No outside slate can win against a third of the vote held by one person, and no buyer can get past the veto. The board's choices (two spin-offs rather than a sale, and the same dual-class structure for NBCUniversal) are the ones that keep that control intact.

- **Where does power lie (shareholders, managers, inside shareholders, another party), and what measure of governance strength supports that?**
  With an inside shareholder who is also management. Measures: 33 1/3% vote on under 1% of the economics (`governance.controlling_holders`); Class B approval rights over any sale (`sources/statements/cmcsa-stated-positions.md`, Control); Roberts as Chairman and Co-CEO. Mitigants: 9 of 11 directors independent, generally no automatic change-of-control payouts, apart from a double-trigger provision in Roberts' own employment agreement (`governance.change_of_control_payouts`), and pay that is 25% exposed to relative TSR, which ranked in the 14th percentile of the S&P 100 over the PSU period that vested (fact `psu-relative-tsr-percentile`).
