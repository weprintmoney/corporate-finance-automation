- **What return on capital did your firm generate last year?**

- **If you have returns on capital over time for your firm, is there a trend in those returns?**
  Down: 9.87% (2022, impairment added back), 10.07%, 9.85%, 8.32% (2025), and 7.85% pro forma (section 8). Operating income fell while invested capital rose with Epic Universe and the Nitel acquisition.

- **How do the returns on capital (equity) compare to the costs of capital (equity)?**
  ROIC 7.85% against a 6.15% cost of capital: +1.70%, EVA $2,953M. ROE 12.26% against an 8.37% cost of equity: +3.88% (section 8). The spread is positive every year but has narrowed by more than half since 2023 (from 3.92 points to 1.70 against today's 6.15%) (exhibit-returns.png).

- **Are the projects in the future likely to look like the projects in the past? Why or why not?**
  No. The past projects were network and franchise investments in a growing broadband market and acquisitions (NBCUniversal, Sky). Future capital goes to fiber upgrades and pricing to defend customers (broadband revenue −5% in Q1 2026), wireless, and theme parks. The defensive spending earns less than the old franchise returns, and after the NBCUniversal spin the parks and studio projects leave with it.
