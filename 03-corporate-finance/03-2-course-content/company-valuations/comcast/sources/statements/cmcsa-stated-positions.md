# Comcast — what the company says, by signal

Every passage below is quoted from a filing in `sources/filings/`. Nothing here was published after 2026-06-30. Ellipses mark cuts; nothing is paraphrased inside quotation marks.

## Control

- **Class B voting.** "Our Class B common stock has a non-dilutable 33 1/3% of the combined voting power of our Class A and Class B common stock. ... The Class B common stock also has separate approval rights over several potentially material transactions, even if they are approved by our Board of Directors or by our other shareholders and even if they might be in the best interests of our other shareholders. These potentially material transactions include mergers or consolidations involving us, transactions (such as a sale of all or substantially all of our assets) or issuances of securities that require shareholder approval..." — `filings/cmcsa-10-k-2026-02-03.md`, Item 1A risk factors (line 886).
- **Who holds it.** "Includes 9,444,375 shares of Class B common stock owned by a limited liability company of which Mr. Roberts is the managing member. The shares of Class B common stock beneficially owned by Mr. Roberts represent 33 1/3% of the combined voting power..." — `filings/cmcsa-def14a-2026-04-24.md`, Security Ownership (line 2018).
- **Change of control.** "We generally do not have any benefits, such as accelerated vesting of equity awards, that are 'triggered' automatically as a result of a 'change in control'..." — `filings/cmcsa-def14a-2026-04-24.md` (line 1215).
- **Co-CEO.** "Michael J. Cavanagh will be appointed Co-Chief Executive Officer of the Company, effective in January 2026. ... he will serve alongside Brian L. Roberts, who will continue as the Company's Chairman of the Board and Co-Chief Executive Officer." — `filings/cmcsa-8-k-2025-09-29-items-502.md`.
- **Second separation, 2026-06-29.** "Comcast Corporation ... today announced its intention to separate into two independent publicly traded companies through a tax-free spin-off of NBCUniversal and Sky. ... Brian L. Roberts will continue to be actively involved in the leadership of Comcast and NBCUniversal ... NBCUniversal will have the same dual-class share structure as Comcast. Comcast expects to retain a stake of up to 19.9% ownership position in NBCUniversal for up to one year after the completion of the spin, which it intends to monetize in a tax-efficient manner over time. Comcast intends to establish a strong investment grade balance sheet for each business..." "The separation is expected to be completed through a tax-free spin-off ... in approximately one year, subject to ... final approval by Comcast's Board of Directors, receipt of tax opinions, regulatory approvals and completion of financing arrangements." — `filings/cmcsa-8-k-2026-06-29-items-801-901.md`, Exhibit 99.1.
