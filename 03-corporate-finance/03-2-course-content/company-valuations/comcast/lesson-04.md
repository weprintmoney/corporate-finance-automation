## Module 1 Step — Questions for This Lesson

- **How many classes of shares exist and what are the voting rights of each?**
  Two. Class A, one vote per share, 3,562.8M shares; Class B, 9.44M shares held by Brian Roberts' LLC with a non-dilutable 33 1/3% of the combined vote and separate approval rights (`governance.share_classes`, `company.shares_basic`).

- **Who are the largest shareholders?**
  By economics, index managers: Vanguard 10.0%, BlackRock 7.4%, State Street 5.1% of Class A (`governance.controlling_holders`). By votes, Roberts (33 1/3%).

- **Is the marginal investor an individual or an institution?**
  An institution (`governance.marginal_investor`).

- **Is that marginal investor diversified?**
  Yes. The three index managers alone hold 22.5% of Class A. A diversified marginal investor means market risk (beta) is the right risk measure for the cost of equity. It also means the price is set by holders who cannot force a sale, which matters for reading the 0.58x price-to-value ratio later (model-output.md section 6: $24.55 against $42.64).
