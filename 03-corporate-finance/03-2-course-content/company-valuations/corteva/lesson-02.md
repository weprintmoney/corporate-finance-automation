- **Whose interests are they most likely to serve?**

Pay rewards Operating EBITDA, margin, free cash flow, RONA and Operating EPS growth (DEF 14A; sources/statements/stated-side-2026-06-29.md S5). These are operating and per-share measures, not size.

- **Where does power lie, and what measure of governance strength supports that?**

Measures: a separate independent chair, 11 of 12 directors independent, double-trigger change-in-control terms only (`governance.change_of_control_payouts`), and stockholder engagement covering about 48% of shares (DEF 14A). There is no controlling holder, dual class, or sponsor. The open question is the post-spin Corteva. Its new CEO came from a private equity partnership (Bernhard Capital) after running Albemarle (8-K 2026-04-14), and its CFO is the officer who led M&A. That is a deal-capable team placed on the half of the company that is losing price.
