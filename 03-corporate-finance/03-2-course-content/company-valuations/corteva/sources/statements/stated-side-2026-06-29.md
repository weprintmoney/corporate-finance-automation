# What Corteva says, by signal

Verbatim passages from filings dated on or before 2026-06-29, grouped by the signal each one speaks to. The latest filing quoted is the 8-K of 2026-06-29.

## Control

- S1. 8-K 2025-10-01, Ex. 99.1: "Board of Directors has unanimously approved a plan to separate the company into two independent, publicly traded companies, one comprising its current Crop Protection business ("New Corteva") and the other comprising its current Seed business ("SpinCo")." ... "Upon separation, current Corteva Chair Greg Page will become Chair of New Corteva; current Corteva CEO Chuck Magro will become CEO of SpinCo." Advisors: "Lazard and Morgan Stanley & Co., LLC are serving as financial advisors."
- S2. 8-K 2026-04-14, Item 5.02: Luke Kissam appointed CEO of New Corteva effective at the spin-off; "Mr. Kissam most recently served as a Partner of Bernhard Capital Partners Management, LP, from January 2021 to September 2023 ... He previously served as President and CEO of Albemarle Corporation". Named executive officers Magro (CEO), Johnson (CFO), Eathington (CTO), O'Connor (Seed) move to SpinCo; Jeff Rudolph, Chief Strategy Officer ("responsible for leading enterprise strategy, mergers and acquisitions"), becomes New Corteva CFO.
- S3. 8-K 2026-04-14, Ex. 10.1 (Kissam letter): spin-off "currently anticipated, but not guaranteed, to occur on October 1, 2026"; Kissam participates in the Change in Control and Executive Severance Plan as "CEO".
- S4. 8-K 2026-06-29, Ex. 99.1: "Corteva will be a company with operational efficiency at its core" ... "New Corteva will be innovation-driven in both its product portfolio and its operating model ... while also running an asset-light, efficient business." Four directors (Grimes, Lutz, Magro, Preete) leave for Vylor's board; "separation on track for 4Q 2026".
- S5. DEF 14A 2026-03-19: beneficial ownership table (Vanguard 12.0%, BlackRock 8.0%, State Street 5.3%; directors and executive officers as a group under 1%); "No single-trigger change in control agreements"; PSUs weighted 50% RONA and 50% three-year cumulative Operating EPS growth; annual bonus 50% Operating EBITDA, 25% Operating EBITDA margin, 25% free cash flow.

## Life cycle

- S6. 8-K 2025-10-01, Ex. 99.1 (New Corteva, crop protection): "Today, while the industry is well supplied – not only in production and capacity, but also with market participants – the market continues to value effective, differentiated technology" ... "New Corteva will nonetheless operate in an industry that will need to compete differently in the future." ... "New Corteva will also benefit from enhanced strategic flexibility, collaboration and partnership across the industry to drive efficiency and value."
- S7. Same release (SpinCo, seed): "SpinCo, a classic growth compounder" ... "capital allocation priorities tailored to its growth model. This will include targeted M&A, sustained investment in R&D".
- S8. 8-K 2026-05-05, Ex. 99.1: Crop Protection "Price declined 2% due to competitive market dynamics in Latin America"; Seed "Price/mix was up 3%".

## Hurdle and returns

- S9. DEF 14A 2026-03-19: "With respect to RONA, the Committee intends to incentive management to drive profitability from its asset footprint through disciplined management of capital investment projects". No hurdle rate or cost of capital is disclosed in any filing read.
- S10. 8-K 2025-10-01, Ex. 99.1 (Magro): "we simplified our portfolio, reduced cost, invested in high-return endeavors".

## Leverage and debt design

- S11. 8-K 2025-10-01, Ex. 99.1: each company to have a "Tailored capital allocation strategy with the flexibility to invest in distinct organic and inorganic growth opportunities, backed by a targeted, investment-grade credit rating"; New Corteva capital "directed towards organic investment ... and disciplined M&A to expand market positions in attractive portfolios or geographies."
- S12. 10-Q filed 2026-05-06, Liquidity: "In establishing the future capital structures of the two independent companies expected to be created through the Proposed Separation, the company evaluated making discretionary contributions to its principal U.S. pension plan. In April 2026, the Board of Directors approved a contribution to the principal U.S. pension plan of approximately $1.5 billion to be made on or before July 31, 2026. The company continues to expect both companies at separation to have strong balance sheets and investment grade credit ratings."
- S13. 8-K 2026-05-05, Ex. 99.1: milestones for 2H 2026 include "Approval of final capital structures" and "Webcasted Investor Day events at New York Stock Exchange on September 15, 2026"; footnote 4: "One-time separation costs do not include deferred asset expenses related to debt issuance costs to be amortized/incurred as future interest payments."
- S14. 10-K filed 2026-02-12, risk factors: "there may be increased borrowing costs associated with the re-allocation or taking on of new debt in connection with the Proposed Separation."
- S15. 10-K filed 2026-02-12, Note 14: foreign currency loans fund "working capital needs of foreign subsidiaries"; the revolving facilities "can be used ... for ... the funding of seasonal working capital needs"; covenant: total indebtedness to total capitalization not to exceed 0.60.

## Payout

- S16. 10-Q filed 2026-05-06: "$3 billion share repurchase program" authorized 2024-11-19; "For the first half of 2026, the company expects repurchases of approximately $500 million under the 2024 Share Buyback Plan"; $2,180 million remaining at 2026-03-31.
- S17. DEF 14A 2026-03-19: "we returned more than $1.5 billion to stockholders during the year via dividends and share repurchases".
