## Module 1 Step — Questions for This Lesson

- **Which company, and why is it a good subject (data available, decisions to judge)?**
  Corteva, Inc. (CTVA), a listed US agricultural inputs company (seed and crop protection), base year FY2025, valued as of 2026-06-30 in a blind backtest. It is a good subject because the board made several large corporate finance decisions in the twelve months before the analysis date: a tax-free spin-off of the Seed business (announced 2025-10-01, target 2026-10-01; `facts` separation-announced, spin-target-date), a $1.5B discretionary pension contribution (`facts` pension-contribution), a cut in the buyback pace, and the choice of a new CEO for the remaining crop protection company. All of them are on file in 10-K, 10-Q, proxy and 8-K documents dated on or before 2026-06-29.

- **Where does it sit on the corporate life cycle, judged by growth, margins, reinvestment and cash use?**
  Mature. Revenue was flat over four years: $17,455M in 2022 and $17,401M in 2025 (`company.history`). Operating income rose from $1,565M to $2,438M, so the gains came from margin, not growth. Reinvestment was negative in 2024 and 2025 (model-output.md section 8 by year: −39.68% and −63.06% of after-tax operating income), and cash returned to shareholders roughly matched free cash flow to equity over 2022–2025 ($5,626M against $5,875M, section 5). Inside the company the two halves are at different stages. Seed operating EBITDA grew from $2,117M to $2,636M between 2023 and 2025, with price/mix up 3% in Q1 2026. Crop Protection EBITDA was flat ($1,374M, $1,272M, $1,350M) with prices down 2% (`facts` seed-op-ebitda-2023-2025, cp-op-ebitda-2023-2025, cp-price-q1-2026).
