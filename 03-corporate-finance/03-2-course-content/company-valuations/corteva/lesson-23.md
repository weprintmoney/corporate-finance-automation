## Cumulative Project — Questions for This Lesson

- **Given its asset/project characteristics, what is the right type of debt for your firm?**
  Seasonal working capital (receivables peak in Q1–Q2) needs short-term, floating funding: commercial paper and revolvers. The long-lived assets (germplasm, trait pipelines, plants) need long-term fixed-rate dollar debt. Revenues are partly in BRL, EUR and ARS, so some foreign-currency debt or hedges fit.

- **What are the actual debt characteristics?**
  Mixed (`debt.rate_type`): $2,200M of fixed senior notes maturing 2026–2033, $667M of floating commercial paper at 2026-03-31, $191M of local-currency loans at 15.65%, $97M of MTNs. Weighted maturity of term debt is 4.3 years (`debt.maturity_years`). Undrawn revolvers total $6,000M (`debt.revolver_undrawn`). Exchange-rate exposure is managed with forward contracts (10-K Note 6).

- **If there is a mismatch, what is the best way to fix it?**
  No serious mismatch. The structure fits the business. The issue is the amount, not the design. At the spin, each company needs its own revolver and CP program and long-dated notes sized to its own cash flows. Crop protection, with more Latin American exposure, is the better candidate for currency-matched debt.
