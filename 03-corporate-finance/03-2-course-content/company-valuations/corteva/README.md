# Corteva, Inc. (CTVA)

Corteva is a US-listed (NYSE: CTVA), Delaware-incorporated seed and crop protection company. It reports in USD with a December fiscal year end. Type `public`, base year FY2025, analysis date 2026-06-30. This is a quick-mode, blind run for the 2026-06-30 backtest (`backtests/2026-06-30/protocol.md`, Amendment 1). Only filings dated on or before 2026-06-30 and market data as of that date were used. The event that shapes the numbers is the announced tax-free spin-off of the Seed business as Vylor, Inc. (announced 2025-10-01, targeted for 2026-10-01). The remaining crop protection business keeps the Corteva name. The FY2025 statements still show one combined company.
