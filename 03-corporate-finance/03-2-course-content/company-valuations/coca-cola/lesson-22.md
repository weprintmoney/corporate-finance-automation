## Cumulative Project — Questions for This Lesson

- **Is your firm under, over or correctly levered?**
  Under-levered: 11.05% against 21% (`model-output.md` section 3) and against peers PepsiCo 21.34% and Keurig Dr Pepper 24.97% (`peers`).

- **If under levered, is it under threat of a hostile acquisition?**
  No. At a 349,662.7 market value no buyer can bid, and the register is index funds plus Berkshire. The discipline that would force more debt is absent.

- **How good are the potential projects that the company can invest in?**
  The core earns 23.81% (`model-output.md` section 8) but needs little capital; the 2026 plan spends about 2,200 of capex (`facts: fcf-guidance-2026`). Acquired brands have earned less (lesson 14). Few good projects need the money.

- **What is the best path that you see for your firm to get from its actual to its optimal debt ratio?**
  Borrow and buy back shares, gradually: the gain is 1,477.2 to 10,121.6 depending on the measure (`model-output.md` sections 6 and 9), and with few projects to fund, returning cash is the use. The company is going the other way: book borrowings fell from 45,492 to 43,890 in Q1 2026 (`facts: q1-2026-total-borrowings`) and buybacks are limited to offsetting dilution (`facts: buyback-2026-intent`).

## Notes

The leverage gap is real and the company is widening it; it is choosing rating over a small value gain.
