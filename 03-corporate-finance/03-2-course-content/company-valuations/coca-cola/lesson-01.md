- **Where does it sit on the corporate life cycle, judged by growth, margins, reinvestment and cash use?**
  Mature. Reported revenue grew 3.69% a year 2022-2025 (`facts: revenue-cagr-2022-2025`), operating income before impairments 7.29% a year (`facts: ebit-ex-impairment-cagr-2022-2025`). Return on capital ran 25.63% to 27.66% (`model-output.md` section 8 by year). Normal reinvestment is small: 9.21% and 16.04% of after-tax operating income in 2022 and 2023; the 68.83% and 74.43% in 2024-2025 are two one-off payments in working capital, the IRS deposit and the fairlife earn-out (`facts: one-off-cash-payments-2024-2025`). Cash use is dividends first: 32,706 over 2022-2025 against 6,248 of buybacks (`facts: dividends-2022-2025`, `buybacks-2022-2025`). It acts its age; the growth narrative sits in pay (EPS and revenue CAGR targets), not in reinvestment.
