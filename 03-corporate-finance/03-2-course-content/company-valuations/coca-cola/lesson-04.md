## Module 1 Step — Questions for This Lesson

- **How many classes of shares exist and what are the voting rights of each?**
  One class, one vote per share (`governance.share_classes`).

- **Who are the largest shareholders?**
  Berkshire Hathaway 9.29% (400M shares), The Vanguard Group 8.61%, BlackRock 7.28%; directors and officers as a group about 0.9% including options (`governance.controlling_holders`).

- **Is the marginal investor an individual or an institution?**
  An institution. Shares outstanding 4,302.5M (`company.shares_basic`); index and active funds trade the stock daily, Berkshire does not.

- **Is that marginal investor diversified?**
  Yes, so market risk (beta) is the right risk measure (`governance.marginal_investor`).
