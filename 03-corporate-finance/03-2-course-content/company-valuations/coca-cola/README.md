# The Coca-Cola Company (KO)

The Coca-Cola Company is a US-listed (`public`) beverage concentrate and brand owner, incorporated in Delaware, reporting in USD with a December year end. Base year FY2025; analysis date 2026-06-30. Quick mode, blind backtest run under `../backtests/2026-06-30/protocol.md` (Amendment 1): only filings published on or before 2026-06-30 and market data as of that date were used.
