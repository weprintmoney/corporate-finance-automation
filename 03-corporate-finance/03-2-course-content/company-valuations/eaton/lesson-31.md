## Cumulative Project — Questions for This Lesson

- **What is the historical growth rate in your company's revenues? EBIT? Net income?**
  2022-2025 a year: revenue 9.8%, EBIT 19.2%, net income 18.4% (`facts.revenue-cagr-2022-2025`, `facts.ebit-cagr-2022-2025`, `facts.net-income-cagr-2022-2025`).

- **What are analysts or managers projecting as an expected growth rate in the future?**
  Management: 2026 organic growth 9-11%, raised in May from 7-9% (`forecast.management_growth`; sources/statements/etn-stated-positions.md S8). Adjusted EPS $13.05-$13.50, GAAP EPS $10.88-$11.33. The GAAP midpoint was cut from $11.82 to $11.105 as the deals brought amortization and interest (`facts.gaap-eps-guidance-2026-feb-mid`, `facts.gaap-eps-guidance-2026-may-mid`). No analyst consensus is in the sources of a blind run.

- **Assuming that your company continues to earn its current ROIC/ROE and maintaining its existing reinvestment rate, what would the expected growth rate be in earnings?**
  Organic only: 16.26% reinvestment × 13.24% ROIC = 2.15% (`facts.fundamental-growth-organic-2025`). Counting 2025 acquisitions as reinvestment: 54.7% × 13.24% = 7.2% (`facts.fundamental-growth-with-acquisitions-2025`). Neither reaches the 10% organic guidance, so either new capital earns far more than the average (the backlog says Electrical does), or growth will need continued acquisitions.
