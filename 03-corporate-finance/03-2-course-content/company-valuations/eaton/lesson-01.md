## Module 1 Step — Questions for This Lesson

- **Which company, and why is it a good subject (data available, decisions to judge)?**
  Eaton Corporation plc, an Irish-domiciled maker of electrical power equipment, aerospace components and vehicle drivetrain parts that files 10-Ks with the SEC (`meta.type` public, base year 2025, analysis date 2026-06-30, blind). It is a good subject because the twelve months before the analysis date are full of corporate finance decisions: $12,671M of acquisitions (`facts.acquisitions-2025-2026-total`), $9,890M of new notes (`facts.notes-issued-2026-03`), buybacks stopped for 2026, and an agreed Reverse Morris Trust that hands the Mobility segment to Dana (`facts.mobility-valuation`).

- **Where does it sit on the corporate life cycle, judged by growth, margins, reinvestment and cash use?**
  A mature, large-cap industrial (revenue $27,448M, `company.revenue`) growing like a younger firm: revenue up 9.8% a year 2022-2025 and EBIT up 19.2% a year (`facts.revenue-cagr-2022-2025`, `facts.ebit-cagr-2022-2025`), with Electrical Americas orders up 42% on a rolling twelve-month basis (`facts.electrical-americas-orders-growth-q1-2026`). Organic reinvestment is light (2025 reinvestment rate 16.26%, model-output.md section 8), but cash use has swung from buybacks to acquisitions. On cash use it is moving from the mature-return stage back toward the growth stage, by buying growth rather than building it.

## Notes

Eaton is a mature industrial that, since mid-2025, has been acting like a growth company. Later lessons test whether the returns justify that.
