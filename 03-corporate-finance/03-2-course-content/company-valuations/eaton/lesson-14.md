## Cumulative Project — Questions for This Lesson

- **What return on capital did your firm generate last year?**
  13.24% after tax on 2025 year-end invested capital of 29,306 (model-output.md section 8); return on equity 20.72%. Goodwill is 53.81% of invested capital. With R&D capitalized over three years, ROIC is 12.75% (alternatives/rd-capitalized-3yr-output.md).

- **If you have returns on capital over time for your firm, is there a trend in those returns?**
  Up, then flat: 8.84% (2022, on estimated capital), 11.32% (2023), 13.34% (2024), 13.24% (2025) (model-output.md section 8). Operating margin rose faster than capital, until the 2025 deals (Fibrebond 1,430) began adding capital.

- **How do the returns on capital (equity) compare to the costs of capital (equity)?**
  ROIC exceeds the 9.69% cost of capital by 3.54 points (EVA 1,038.8); ROE exceeds the 10.45% cost of equity by 10.28 points (model-output.md section 8). That holds on the 2025 base. Add the 11,079 spent on acquisitions in Q1 2026 and invested capital becomes 40,385; 2025 after-tax EBIT on that base is 9.6%, at the cost of capital, before counting what Boyd and Ultra earn (`facts.pro-forma-invested-capital`).

- **Are the projects in the future likely to look like the projects in the past? Why or why not?**
  No. Past returns came from organic growth on a mostly written-down asset base. The new projects are acquisitions at full prices. In their first weeks Boyd Thermal earned segment profit at an annual rate of about 4.8% of its price and Ultra PCS about 4.6%, before tax and before amortization of the $6.4B of acquired intangibles (`facts.boyd-annualized-segment-profit-yield`, `facts.ultra-annualized-segment-profit-yield`, `facts.boyd-intangibles`). Those are stub periods of 19 and 67 days, so they are weak evidence, but even a doubling leaves the deals below a 9.69% hurdle on an after-tax basis.

## Notes

Returns were well above the hurdle through 2025. The 2026 acquisitions reset the base, and the early evidence is that they earn below the hurdle.
