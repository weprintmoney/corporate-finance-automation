# Eaton Corporation plc (ETN) — Lesson 02: The Objective: Utopia and Let Down

### Lesson 02 — The Objective: Utopia and Let Down

Quick mode: only the question-bank entries with a `signals` field are answered here. No transcript-layer entries carry a signal, so none are listed.

## Module 1 Step — Questions for This Lesson

- **Who sits on the board of directors?**
  Eleven nominees (`governance.board`): ten independent non-employee directors and the CEO, Paulo Ruiz. Gregory Page, a director since 2003, has been non-executive chairman since 2025-06-01, when Craig Arnold retired as chairman and CEO (`facts.ceo-start`). Two directors are sitting executives elsewhere (Robert Pragada, CEO of Jacobs; Andre Schulten, CFO of Procter & Gamble).

- **Whose interests are they most likely to serve?**
  Diffuse institutional shareholders. No holder exceeds 10% (Vanguard 9.43%, BlackRock 7.04%; insiders 0.10%; `governance.controlling_holders`), so no blockholder can steer the board. With a split chair and CEO and an independent board, the board's own reference point is total shareholder return against peers, which is what the long-term pay plan measures (sources/statements/etn-stated-positions.md S1).

- **Where does power lie (shareholders, managers, inside shareholders, another party), and what measure of governance strength supports that?**
  With management and the board, checked by index-fund holders. Signs of reasonable governance: annual majority-vote elections, a separate independent chairman, and all committees chaired by independent directors. A sign of weakness: management's pay is set on Adjusted EPS (2025) and Adjusted EBITA plus organic growth (2026), which exclude acquisition charges and amortization of acquired intangibles, and nothing in pay charges for the capital acquisitions use (`governance.objectives`). Executives have double-trigger change-of-control agreements at 3x salary plus target bonus (`governance.change_of_control_payouts`).

## Notes

Power sits with a new management team (CEO since 2025-06-01, CFO since 2026-03-02, `facts.cfo-start`) and an independent board. The pay design matters for the returns signal later.
