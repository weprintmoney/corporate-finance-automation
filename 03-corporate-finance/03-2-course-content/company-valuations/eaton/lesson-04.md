### Lesson 04 — Define and Measure Risk

## Module 1 Step — Questions for This Lesson

- **How many classes of shares exist and what are the voting rights of each?**
  One class of ordinary shares, one vote each (`governance.share_classes`).

- **Who are the largest shareholders?**
  Vanguard 9.43% and BlackRock 7.04% (13G/As as reported in the 2026 proxy); directors and officers 0.10% (`governance.controlling_holders`).

- **Is the marginal investor an individual or an institution?**
  An institution (`governance.marginal_investor`). Shares outstanding are 388.3M (`company.shares_basic`).

- **Is that marginal investor diversified?**
  Yes. Index funds and large active managers hold diversified portfolios, so only market risk (beta) should be priced. That supports a CAPM cost of equity with a bottom-up beta (lesson 10).

## Notes

Diversified institutional marginal investor: use market beta, not total beta.
