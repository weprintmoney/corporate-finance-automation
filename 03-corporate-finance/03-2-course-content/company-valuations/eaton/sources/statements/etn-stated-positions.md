# What Eaton says, by signal

Verbatim passages from Eaton's own filings, each with the file it comes from in `sources/filings/`. The latest passage was filed on 2026-06-11. Nothing filed after 2026-06-30 is quoted.

## S1. Control and objectives

- **Board leadership** (`etn-def14a-2026-03-13.md`, Leadership Structure): "On May 31, 2025, Craig Arnold, who had served as Eaton's Chairman and CEO since June 2016, retired and Paulo Ruiz was appointed CEO, effective June 1, 2025. ... the Board determined that it was appropriate to separate the positions of Chairman of the Board and CEO and appointed Gregory Page to serve as non-executive Chairman of the Board."
- **Pay design** (`etn-def14a-2026-03-13.md`, 2023-2025 ESIP Performance Criteria): "We and the Committee believe a short-term plan with earnings growth and operating cash flow metrics (via the Adjusted EPS and Adjusted OCF goals) complemented by a TSR-driven long-term plan is an effective combination that correlates with shareholder value creation."
- **Adjusted EPS definition** (`etn-def14a-2026-03-13.md`, 2025 EIC): "Adjusted EPS is defined as net income, excluding acquisition and divestiture charges, restructuring program charges, and intangible asset amortization expense."
- **2026 pay metrics** (`etn-8-k-2026-03-02-items-502.md`, Item 5.02): "The Committee selected Adjusted Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Amortization, and Depreciation; Adjusted Operating Cash Flow; and Organic Growth as the metrics for the 2026 Program."
- **CFO change-of-control terms** (`etn-8-k-2026-03-02-items-502-901.md`, Item 5.02): on a qualifying termination after a change in control, "a cash payment equal to three times his base salary plus target annual incentive opportunity."

## S2. Life cycle and strategy

- **Business description** (`etn-10-k-2026-02-26.md`, Item 1): "We are capitalizing on the megatrends of the electrification, digitalization, and the reindustrialization of and growth of megaprojects in North America and increased global infrastructure spending, all of which are expanding our end markets and positioning Eaton for growth for years to come."
- **Portfolio management** (`etn-10-k-2026-02-26.md`, Note 2): "The Company is focused on deploying its capital toward businesses that provide opportunities for above-market growth, strong returns, and align with secular trends and its power management strategies."
- **Mobility separation** (`etn-8-k-2026-06-11-items-701-801-901.md`, Exhibit 99.1): "Upon closing of the transaction, Eaton will operate a more focused portfolio concentrated on its Electrical and Aerospace businesses ... The separation is expected to be immediately accretive to Eaton's organic growth rate and operating margins upon closing." Closing is "expected in the first quarter of 2027, subject to receipt of Dana shareholder approval, receipt of required regulatory clearances, and customary closing conditions."

## S3. Hurdle rate and risk

- No hurdle rate or cost of capital is disclosed. The goodwill test uses "the applicable weighted-average cost of capital" without giving it (`etn-10-k-2026-02-26.md`, Note 1 goodwill).
- **Ratings** (`etn-10-k-2026-02-26.md`, Liquidity): "Standard & Poor's A-/A-2 Stable outlook; Moody's A3/P-2 Stable outlook."

## S4. Returns

- **Acquisition rationale** (`etn-8-k-2026-05-05-items-202-901.md`, Exhibit 99): "the company also closed $11 billion of value-enhancing strategic acquisitions, including Boyd Thermal ... and Ultra PCS Limited ... These acquisitions reinforce Eaton's disciplined M&A strategy—deploying capital to invest for growth by acquiring differentiated technologies in high‑growth, high‑margin markets that support long‑term value creation."
- No return-on-capital target appears in the 10-K, the proxy or the earnings releases.

## S5. Leverage

- No leverage or net-debt target is stated in the 10-K, 10-Q or releases.
- **Use of RMT cash** (`etn-8-k-2026-06-11-items-701-801-901.md`, Exhibit 99.1): "Eaton expects to deploy the approximately $1.1 billion cash distribution from the transaction consistent with its existing capital allocation framework and priorities, including repayment of outstanding indebtedness."
- **Covenant** (`etn-10-k-2026-02-26.md`, Liquidity): "the most restrictive financial covenant provides that the ratio of secured debt ... to adjusted consolidated net worth ... may not exceed 10%." The February 2026 term loan added "The ratio of Consolidated Debt to Consolidated Capitalization shall not exceed 0.65:1.00" (`etn-8-k-2026-02-06-items-101-203-901.md`, Section 5.07); that facility was terminated undrawn on 2026-03-06.

## S6. Debt design

- **Purpose of the 2026 notes** (`etn-8-k-2026-03-10-items-102-203-901.md`, Item 2.03): "intend to use the net proceeds from the respective Notes for general corporate purposes, including to consummate the previously disclosed acquisition of Boyd Thermal."
- **Rate risk** (`etn-10-k-2026-02-26.md`, Item 7A): "a 100 basis point increase in short-term interest rates would have decreased the Company's net, pre-tax interest expense by $8 million"; "a hypothetical, 100 basis point increase in interest rates ... the market value of the Company's debt, in aggregate, would decrease by $617 million."
- **Commercial paper** (`etn-10-q-2026-05-05.md`, Note 8): "$4,000 million commercial paper program, of which $2,497 million was outstanding on March 31, 2026."

## S7. Payout

- **Buybacks paused** (`etn-10-q-2026-05-05.md`, Note on equity): "The Company does not intend to pursue share repurchases in 2026 due to the acquisition of Boyd Thermal on March 12, 2026."
- **Authorization** (`etn-10-k-2026-02-26.md`, Note on equity): "At December 31, 2025, there is $7,597 million still available for share repurchase under the 2025 Program." The 2025 Program runs three years from 2025-02-27.
- **Dividend** (`etn-10-k-2026-02-26.md`, Note on equity): "On February 26, 2026, Eaton's Board of Directors declared a quarterly dividend of $1.10 per ordinary share, a 6% increase over the dividend paid in the fourth quarter of 2025."

- **2026 guidance, May** (`etn-8-k-2026-05-05-items-202-901.md`, Exhibit 99): organic growth 9-11%; segment margins 24.1-24.5%; EPS $10.88-$11.33; adjusted EPS $13.05-$13.50. "Raises 2026 Organic Growth Guidance to 10% from 8% at the Midpoint."
- **Mobility price** (`etn-8-k-2026-06-11-items-701-801-901.md`, Exhibit 99.1): "The transaction values Eaton's Mobility Group at approximately $5.1 billion. This represents a multiple of 8.3x 2026 estimated pro forma adjusted EBITDA."
