## Why these companies

These are the twelve companies Jefferies used in its fairness opinion, less four (deal document `../deal-docs/swi-defm14c-2025-merger.md`, "Selected Public Companies Analysis"):
- **Check Point, TeamViewer and Trend Micro** were dropped because they file outside the US on different accounting standards.
- **Informatica** was dropped because it has since been acquired, and its price history is no longer available from free sources.

They are mature security and infrastructure software companies whose growth (−9% to 10%, except OpenText) brackets SolarWinds' 5.0%.

Datadog, Dynatrace and PagerDuty were considered, as the closest observability competitors, and rejected. Their revenue grew 24–26% (Datadog, Dynatrace) and 9% with operating losses (PagerDuty). That made their multiples a measure of growth SolarWinds did not have.

## Caveats

- **OpenText:** D&A is not tagged in company facts, so its EBITDA is n/a and it drops out of the EV/EBITDA median only. Its 28.6% growth is the Micro Focus acquisition, not organic growth.
- **Negative EBITDA:** NetScout's fiscal 2024 operating loss includes a goodwill impairment. Negative EBITDA and earnings drop out of those medians (the model skips negative denominators).
- **Progress:** tags only depreciation as D&A, so the tool added acquired-intangible amortization (see the tag list).
