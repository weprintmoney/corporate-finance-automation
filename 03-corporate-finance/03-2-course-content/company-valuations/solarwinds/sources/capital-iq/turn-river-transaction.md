# Turn/River Management acquisition of SolarWinds — Capital IQ transaction record

**Source:** S&P Capital IQ, Transactions > M&A/Private Placements > transaction IQTR1930453238 (announced 2025-02-07, closed 2025-04-16). Pulled 2026-10-05. CIQ notes that some data points are calculated by CIQ rather than quoted from press releases. Primary documents are in `../sec-filings/` (8-Ks and the Schedule 14C information statement).

## Deal values (this settles the $4.4B vs $4.68B question)

| Item | $ mm |
|---|---|
| Offer per share | $18.50 cash |
| **Total consideration to shareholders (implied equity value)** | **3,420.88** |
| Options / rights / warrants | 1.99 |
| Net assumed liabilities (total debt 1,255.95 minus cash 259.32) | 996.6 |
| **Implied enterprise value (total net transaction value)** | **4,419.50** |
| Add back target cash & ST investments | 259.32 |
| **Total gross transaction value** (the 4,678.82 shown in the CIQ summary) | **4,678.82** |

So the headline $4.4B is enterprise value, equity value is **$3.42B**, and CIQ's "transaction size" of $4,678.82M is a gross figure that adds the target's cash back. Do not use 4,678.82 as either EV or equity value. Implied diluted share count = 3,420.88 / 18.50 ≈ **184.9M**, consistent with the README correction (185.0M fully diluted vs 171.6M basic).

## Financing

| Source | $ mm |
|---|---|
| Senior debt (committed) | 2,950.00 |
| Equity investment (cash from the company's own balance sheet per the CIQ synopsis) | 225.00 |
| Sub-debt / mezzanine | – |

From the CIQ synopsis of the debt commitment letter: **first lien term facility $2,225.0M, revolver $200.0M, second lien term facility $525.0M**. Equity comes from Turn/River funds and co-investors, plus $225.0M of SolarWinds' cash. This is the **post-LBO capital structure**: net debt rises from about $1.0B to roughly $2.7B+, versus 4.4x debt/EBITDA before the deal. Useful for a "what would WACC look like under the new owners" section.

## Corporate profile

CIQ lists SolarWinds Corporation as a **private company**, primary industry Systems Software, Austin TX, 2,107 employees, founded 1999. Its Mar-03-2025 acquisition of Squadcast (from DNX Capital) is the last deal it made as a buyer, and its other recent buys include Monalytic (2022-01-19).
