## Notes

- **Gap between the two Treasury rates.** The daily close is 0.11 percentage points below the monthly figure, under the skill's 0.25-point threshold, so the cost of capital is shown at the daily close only.
- **Why the spread is zero.** Before the downgrade, Damodaran's workbook had one risk-free rate and one implied premium, so the "over the dollar risk-free rate" and "over the Treasury" premiums are the same number. The US default spread was zero because the US was rated Aaa.
- **Ratings table, betas and country premiums.** These are the January 2025 editions from Damodaran's archive (`ratings24.xls`, `betas24.xls`, `ctryprem24.xlsx`). In each one, the file name's year is the year the data describes.
