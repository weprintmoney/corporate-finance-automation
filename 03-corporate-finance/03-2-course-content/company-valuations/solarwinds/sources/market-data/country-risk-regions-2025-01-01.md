# Regional equity risk premiums and the US marginal tax rate, January 2025

Extracted on 2026-10-25 from Damodaran's January 2025 country risk workbook, `https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pc/archives/ctryprem24.xlsx` (the same file as `country-risk-2025-01-01.json`). That JSON holds only the per-country rows; these sheets feed `country.exposures` and `market.tax_rate_marginal`.

In this workbook the mature-market premium is 4.33% ("Enter the current risk premium for a mature equity market", ERPs by country sheet). A region's country risk premium = its equity risk premium − 4.33%.
