# The Coca-Cola Company (KO)

Quick-mode, blind `/value-company` run as of 2026-06-30, part of the backtest in `../backtests/2026-06-30/protocol.md`.

## Context

Coca-Cola is a Delaware company listed on the NYSE (one share class). It reports in US dollars with a December year end, and is analysed in US dollars. Base year: FY2025. Type: `public`. Three events change how the numbers read:
- The fairlife earn-out of $6,173M was paid in March 2025, and the $6.0B IRS Tax Litigation Deposit was paid in September 2024. Both depress cash from operations.
- The African bottling operations are held for sale to CCHBC, with closing expected by the end of 2026.
- A CEO succession took effect on 2026-03-31 (Henrique Braun as CEO, James Quincey as executive chairman).

The appeal of the 2007–2009 tax case is pending at the Eleventh Circuit, and the 10-K puts the potential further liability at about $14B.
