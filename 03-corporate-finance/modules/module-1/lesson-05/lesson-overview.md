# Lesson 5 — The Risk Free Rate

## Overview

While risk is part of every business, the first step in assessing what you need to make as compensation for being exposed to risk is estimating the return that you can generate on a riskless investment. That return is the risk free rate, and it will vary across currencies and over time. This lesson specifies the criteria that make an investment risk free and then uses those criteria to estimate risk free rates in different currencies, examining why risk free rates vary across currencies and why analysis should be currency invariant.

**Learning objectives:**
- Specify the criteria that make an investment risk free
- Estimate risk free rates in different currencies
- Understand why risk free rates vary across currencies and how to keep analysis currency invariant
