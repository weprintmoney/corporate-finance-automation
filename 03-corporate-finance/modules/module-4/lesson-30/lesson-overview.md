# Lesson 30 — Cash Flows

## Overview

The intrinsic value of a company is a function of its cash flows and how much risk there is in them, with the former captured in the magnitude of the cash flows and the latter in a discount rate. By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

**Learning objectives:**
- Adapt the costs of equity and capital that you may have computed for investment analysis already, for valuation, and measure the free cash flow for either the firm or to equity investors in recent periods.
