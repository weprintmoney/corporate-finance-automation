# Lesson 17 — The Trade Off

## Overview

As companies look outside their domestic markets for investment opportunities, we have to be able to not only deal with different currencies, when doing investment analysis, but also with the uncertainty that is inherent with making decisions in an ever-changing world. By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

**Learning objectives:**
- Compute investment returns for a project in alternative currencies and develop techniques for dealing with uncertainty in decision-making (from breakevens to scenario analysis to simulations).

## Project Questions

- What are the different kinds or types of financing that this company has used to raise funds? Where do they fall in the continuum between debt and equity?
- How large, in qualitative or quantitative terms, are the advantages to this company from using debt? How large, in qualitative or quantitative terms, are the disadvantages to this company from using debt? From the qualitative trade off, does this firm look like it has too much or too little debt?"
