## Instructions

### Introduction

This is an applied corporate finance class and the test will be whether you can apply the tools that we develop in class to real companies in real time.

### Instruction

Here is what I would like you to do: find a company to value. You can pick any company that you can get financial statements for at least two years (the company can be a private business, if you want). Don’t worry about picking the wrong company; there is no such thing, and you can change your company midway through the semester if you want.

Each session, I will list what I would like you to do for your company - please follow the list of questions on each lesson under "Cumulative Project". Thus, when we talk about equity risk premiums, you will estimate the equity risk premium for your company. Over the course of the semester, you will come up with a full corporate finance assessment of your company, reflecting what it does well, what it does badly and what you think it can do differently.

### The final deliverable

The final valuation will take the form of project report (short, about 7 - 9 pages) and I will grade that project as part of your grade. Please note you are only allowed one submission - please make sure your complete assignment is submitted at that time. This project is due on December 11, 2026, 11:59 PM ET.
