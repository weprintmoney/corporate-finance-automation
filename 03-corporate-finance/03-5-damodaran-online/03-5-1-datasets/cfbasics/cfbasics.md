# Cfbasics

Source: https://www.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pc/datasets/cfbasics.xls

Sheets: Variables & FAQ, Industry Averages

## Variables & FAQ

| End Game | Summary of inputs that are needed to estimate cash flows. |
|---|---|
|  |  |
| Variable | Explanation |
| Number of firms | Number of firms in the indusry grouping. |
| Pre-tax Operating Margin (adjusted for leases) | Aggregated operating income, with adjustment for leases being capitalized (with lease expenses added back and lease asset depreciation added back), as a percent of aggregated revenues across all companies in grouping. |
| Pre-tax Operating Margin (unadjusted) | Aggregated operating income,as stated in financials, as a percent of aggregated revenues across all companies in grouping. |
| Effective Tax Rate | Effective tax rate in the most recent 12 months. |
| Non-cash working capital as percent of revenues | Aggregated value of (Current Assets - Cash) - (Current liabilities - Short term debt in Current liabilities), as percent of revenues. |
| Net Cap Ex/ Revenues | Aggregated value of (Capital Expenditures minus Depreciaiton), as percent of revenues. |
| Sales/Capital | Aggregated revenues across firms in group/ Aggregated invested capital across firms in group; Invested capital = Book value of equity + Book value of debt - Cash. |
