# Insholdchina

Source: https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pc/datasets/insholdChina.xls

Sheets: Variables & FAQ, Industry Averages

## Variables & FAQ

| End Game | Look at percent of stock held by insiders and institutions, to get a measure of control and corporate governance. |
|---|---|
|  |  |
| Variable | Explanation |
| Number of firms | Number of firms in the industry grouping. |
| CEO holdings | Average of CEO holdings, as percent of all outstanding stock, across companies in the group. |
| Insitutional holdings | Average of holdings by institutions (mutual and pension funds), as percent of all outstanding stock, across companies in the group. |
| Insider holdings | Average of holdings by insiders (managers, directors, founders), as percent of all outstanding stock, across companies in the group. |
