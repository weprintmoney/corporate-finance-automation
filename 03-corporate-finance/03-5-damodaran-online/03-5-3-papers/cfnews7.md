value added for your firm. To compare your company's return spread to that of the sector, you can use my EVA dataset on my web site:

http://www.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New\_Home\_Page/data.html

When you do measure your company's return on equity or capital, don't be surprised by weird looking numbers that don't conform to your priors. The return on equity (capital) is an accounting number, and to the extent that both the numerator (earnings) and denominator (book value) can be skewed by accounting decisions. To provide just one illustration, big buybacks and write offs can make book value of equity and even capital negative. If that happens, the accounting returns become meaningless, and you have to look for other clues to project quality.
