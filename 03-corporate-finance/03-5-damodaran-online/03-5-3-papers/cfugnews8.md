## *Miscellaneous FAQs*

*When is the next quiz and what will it cover?*

The next quiz is on Monday, April 4, and it will cover the cost of capital calculation and the investment return section of the notes (from page 145 or so to the end of part I). We should be done with this by the next two sessions and you can start working through practice problems, whenever you get a chance. In the book, the relevant chapters are the last part of chapter 4, chapter 5 and chapter 6.

*What do I do if a 10-K comes out in the next week or two for my firm?*

I would suggest that you update your analysis. In fact, I would strongly recommend that when you build spreadsheets for your firm, you set up a separate input page, where you can update numbers and have them reflected in the entire analysis.
