## **Problem 6**

a. Standard deviation of portfolio entirely composed of stocks = 20%

Variance of mixed portfolio = .202 (.90)2 + .152 (.10)2 + 2(.20)(.15)(.10)(.9)(.1) = .0332

b. I would not add art to my portfolio. The expected return is less than the riskfree rate and I would gain a much bigger gain by putting 10% of my money in the riskless asset (with a zero standard deviation and a correlation of zero with the market) than I would by putting it in art. For art to qualify as a good investment, the expected return would have to be greater than 6% or the correlation would have to be negative.
