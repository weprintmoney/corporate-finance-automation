## Estimating a default spread for a country or sovereign entity

### Market Based estimates

#### Sovereign Bond spread

1. 1. Find a bond issued by the country, denominated in US\$ or Euros.
2. 2. Compute the default spread by comparing to US treasury bond (if US \$) or German Euro bond (if Euros).

#### CDS Market

1. 1. Find a 10-year CDS for the country (if one exists)
2. 2. This is your default spread.
