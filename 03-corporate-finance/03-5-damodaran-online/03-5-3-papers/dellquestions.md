In

- (1) When

   the

   stockholders.

   the 
   founder/owner

   the 
   problems,

   some

   there 
   will 
   be

   separation 
   between 
   the 
   two. 
   The

   corporate

   the 
   other… Lenders

   covenants,

   to 
   the 
   firm.

   firm 
   is

   Without

   debating 
   whether 
   than 
   market 
   price 
   is 
   fair 
   and

   publicly 
   traded 
   companies 
   are 
   influenced by 
   market 
   judgments 
   that 
   can 
   then 
   alter 
   how 
   they 
   are 
   run. The 
   question 
   then

   whether 
   these 
   influences 
   are 
   positive 
   or 
   negative..
- (4) Every 
   business

   social 
   costs, 
   and 
   balancing 
   the 
   interests 
   of 
   the 
   owners
