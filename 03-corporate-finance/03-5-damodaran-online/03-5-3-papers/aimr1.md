## Estimating Synthetic Ratings and cost of debt

<sup>n</sup> The rating for a firm can be estimated using the financial characteristics of the firm. In its simplest form, the rating can be estimated from the interest coverage ratio

Interest Coverage Ratio = EBIT / Interest Expenses

<sup>n</sup> Amazon.com has negative operating income; this yields a negative interest coverage ratio, which should suggest a low rating. We computed an average interest coverage ratio of 2.82 over the next 5 years. This yields an average rating of BBB for Amazon.com for the first 5 years.

#### Estimating the cost of debt

<sup>n</sup> The synthetic rating for Amazon.com is BBB. The default spread for BBB rated bonds is 1.50%

■ Pre-tax cost of debt = Riskfree Rate + Default spread

This rule becomes even more critical when valuing companies that are evolving and growing rapidly.

## Relative Valuation

#### What is relative valuation?

- <sup>n</sup> In relative valuation, the value of an asset is compared to the values assessed by the market for similar or comparable assets. <sup>n</sup> To do relative valuation then,
  - we need to identify comparable assets and obtain market values for these assets
  - convert these market values into standardized values, since the absolute prices cannot be compared.
  - compare the standardized value or multiple for the asset being analyzed to the standardized values for comparable asset, controlling for any differences.

## The Four Steps to Understanding Multiples

- <sup>n</sup> Define the multiple
- Multiple can be defined in different ways by users. <sup>n</sup> Describe the multiple
- Too many people who use a multiple have no idea what its cross sectional distribution is. <sup>n</sup> Analyze the multiple
- It is critical that we understand the fundamentals that drive each multiple. <sup>n</sup> Apply the multiple
  - Defining the comparable universe and controlling for differences .

#### Price Sales Ratio: Definition

<sup>n</sup> The price/sales ratio is the ratio of the market value of equity to the sales.

<sup>n</sup> Price/ Sales= Market Value of Equity Total Revenues

<sup>n</sup> Consistency Tests

- The price/sales ratio is internally inconsistent, since the market value of equity is divided by the total revenues of the firm.

#### Price/Sales Ratio: Determinants

<sup>n</sup> The price/sales ratio of a stable growth firm can be estimated beginning with a 2-stage equity valuation model:

<sup>n</sup> Dividing both sides by the sales per share:

$$P_0 = \frac{DPS_1}{r - g_n}$$

$$\frac{P_0}{\text{Sales}_0} = PS = \frac{\text{Net Profit Margin} * \text{Payout Ratio} * (1 + g_n)}{r - g_n}$$

#### In summary...

<sup>n</sup> If sectors are loosely defined (as is the internet sector) to include retailers, software producers, publishers and service companies, multiples have to be used with caution. <sup>n</sup> Differences in multiples for companies that derive almost all of their value from future growth are better explained by looking at variables that are likely be correlated with future growth than by looking at current earnings or cash flows.
