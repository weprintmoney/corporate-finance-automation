## **Quiz 2: Equity Valuation**

Answer all questions and show necessary work. Please be brief. This is an open-book, open-notes exam.

1. Carvel Enterprises is a publicly traded firm, with 100 million shares outstanding, trading at \$41/share. The firm has no debt outstanding, and a cash balance of \$750 million. The expected free cash flow to the firm next year is \$175 million, growing at 3% a year in perpetuity, and the cost of capital is 8%. You can assume that the market is valuing the operating assets correctly, but that it is discounting the cash balance, because of lack of trust in management. Estimate the **percentage discount that the market is attaching to the cash balance**. (2 points) (2 points) i. 2. You are reviewing the valuation of Sola Chemicals, a chemical company with two cross holdings – a 60% holding in Compress Steel and a 10% holding in Magna Mining, with the former holding being fully consolidated. The company's most recent balance sheet shows the following (in \$ millions):

| Fixed Assets             | \$1,600 | Debt                               | \$0.00     |
|--------------------------|---------|------------------------------------|------------|
| Cash                     | \$100   | Equity                             | \$1,700.00 |
| Non-cash Current Assets  | \$250   | Minority Interest (Compress Steel) | \$300.00   |
| Minority Holding (Magna) | \$50    |                                    |            |

The analyst has arrived at an equity value of \$1.5 billion, and while you agree with much of what she has done to get to that value, you believe that she should have used an estimated market value for the cross holdings, rather than book value. If steel companies trade at 2.5 times book value and mining companies at 1.5 times book value, **re-estimate the value of equity in the firm**. **(2 points)**

3. You have been provided with the expected free cashflows to the firm, for the next two years, with present values, for Fergus Inc., a manufacturing firm, for the next most recent year and the next two years in \$ millions:

|            | Base year | 1         | 2         |
|------------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| EBIT (1-t) | \$ 100.00 | \$ 110.00 | \$ 121.00 |
| FCFF       | \$ 20.00  | \$ 22.00  | \$ 24.20  |

Assuming that the firm can maintain its current return on capital and cost of capital (used in computing the present value) in perpetuity, and that the expected growth rate in perpetuity is 2.50**%, estimate the terminal value (at the end of year 2)**. (3 points)
