## *Miscellaneous FAQs*

*My company pays no dividends. Does this mean that I don't analyze its dividend policy?*

Not paying dividends is also a dividend policy. You will have to look at whether this policy makes sense, especially if the company is generating positive free cashflows to equity each year.

*Where can I find information on stock buybacks?*

The best source for stock buybacks is the statement of cashflows. It will be in the financing section.
