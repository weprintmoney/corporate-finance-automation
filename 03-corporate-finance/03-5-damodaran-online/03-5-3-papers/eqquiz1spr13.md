## **Quiz 1: Valuation**

Answer all questions and show necessary work. Please be brief. This is an open books, open notes exam.

- 1. You are reviewing the valuation of Xtra Inc., a stable growth, chemical company. The analyst has arrived at a value of \$2.4 billion for the equity in the firm, by discounting cash flows (after interest expenses and debt cash flows) at a discount rate of 8%, using a 2% growth rate in perpetuity; he used the cost of capital as the discount rate. If Xtra Inc. has no cash balance, a debt to capital ratio of 20% and an after-tax cost of debt of 4%, estimate the correct value of equity in the firm. (3 points)

The company reported \$150 million in capital expenditures during the year. Putting your valuation knowledge to use, you have also uncovered the following:

- The company has expected lease commitments of \$80 million each year for the next 5 years. The pre-tax cost of debt is 5%.
- The amortizable life of the R&D is 3 years, and based on the R&D expenses for the last 3 years, you have computed that the amortization in the most recent year would have been \$100 million and that the unamortized R&D from prior years would be \$250 million today.

Estimate the corrected after-tax operating income for the most recent year. (3 points)
