# **BIAS AND PRECONCEPTIONS: THE BIGGEST BARRIER TO VALUATION**

Session 2

# BIAS: THE SOURCES

- ▪ **The power of the subconscious:** We are human, after all, and as a consequence are susceptible to
  - ▪ Herd behavior: For instance, there is the “market price” magnet in valuation, where estimates of intrinsic value move towards the market price with each iteration.
  - ▪ Hindsight bias: If you know the outcome of a sequence of events, it will affect your valuation. (That is why teaching valuation with cases is an exercise in futility)
- ▪ **The power of suggestion:** Hearing what others think a company is worth will color your thinking, and if you view those others as more informed/smarter than you are, you will be influenced even more.
- ▪ **The power of money:** If you have an economic stake in the outcome of a valuation, bias will almost always follow.
  - ▪ Corollary 1: Your bias in a valuation will be directly proportional to who pays you to do the valuation and how much you get paid.
  - ▪ Corollary 2: You will be more biased when valuing a company where you already have a position (long or short) in the company.
