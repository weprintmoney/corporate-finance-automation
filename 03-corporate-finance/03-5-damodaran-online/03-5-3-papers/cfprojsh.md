# Corporate Finance The Project

Aswath Damodaran

# What this project involves…

n You should consider this project a live lab experiment that you will be doing in class for the next few sessions. While I will try to apply the principles of corporate finance to the companies I have chosen - Disney, Bookscape… etc. you will be applying the same principles to your company. n Many of the numbers will be worked out in class as you go through the material. n After the course is over, you should write a corporate financial analysis of your company, using the numbers that you have generated in class.

#### I. Stockholder Analysis

n Who is the average investor in this stock? (Individual or pension fund, taxable or tax-exempt, small or large, domestic or foreign) n Who is the marginal investor in this stock?

## II. Risk and Return

n What is the risk profile of your company? (How much overall risk is there in this firm? Where is this risk coming from (market, firm, industry or currency)? How is the risk profile changing?) n What is the performance profile of an investment in this company? What return would you have earned investing in this company's stock? Would you have under or out performed the market? How much of the performance can be attributed to management? n How risky is this company's equity? Why? What is its cost of equity? n How risky is this company's debt? What is its cost of debt? n What is this company's current cost of capital?

## V. Optimal Capital Structure

- n Based upon the cost of capital approach, what is the optimal debt ratio for your firm? n Bringing in reasonable constraints into the decision process, what would your recommended debt ratio be for this firm? n Does your firm have too much or too little debt
  - relative to the sector?
  - relative to the market?

# VI. Mechanics of Moving to the Optimal

- n If your firm's actual debt ratio is different from its "recommended" debt ratio, how should they get from the actual to the optimal? In particular,
  - should they do it gradually over time or should they do it right now?
- should they alter their existing mix (by buying back stock or retiring debt) or should they take new projects with debt or equity? n What type of financing should this firm use? In particular,
  - should it be short term or long term?
  - what currency should it be in?
  - what special features should the financing have?
