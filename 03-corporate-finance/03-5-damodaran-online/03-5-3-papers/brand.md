# Value/Sales Ratio: An Example

■ Consider, for example, the Value/Sales ratio of Coca Cola. The company had the following characteristics:

After-tax Operating Margin = 18.56%      Sales/BV of Capital = 1.67

Return on Capital =  $1.67 * 18.56\% = 31.02\%$ 

Reinvestment Rate = 65.00% in high growth; 20% in stable growth;

Expected Growth =  $31.02\% * 0.65 = 20.16\%$  (Stable Growth Rate = 6%)

Length of High Growth Period = 10 years

Cost of Equity = 12.33%       $E/(D+E) = 97.65\%$ 

After-tax Cost of Debt = 4.16%       $D/(D+E) = 2.35\%$ 

Cost of Capital =  $12.33\% (.9765) + 4.16\% (.0235) = 12.13\%$ 

$$\frac{\text{Value of Firm}_0}{\text{Sales}_0} = .1856 * \left[ \frac{(1-.65)(1.2016) * \left(1 - \frac{(1.2016)^{10}}{(1.1213)^{10}}\right)}{.1213-.2016} + \frac{(1-.20)(1.2016)^{10} * (1.06)}{(.1213-.06)(1.1213)^{10}} \right] = 6.10$$

# The value of a brand name

<sup>n</sup> One of the critiques of traditional valuation is that is fails to consider the value of brand names and other intangibles. <sup>n</sup> The approaches used by analysts to value brand names are often adhoc and may significantly overstate or understate their value. <sup>n</sup> One of the benefits of having a well-known and respected brand name is that firms can charge higher prices for the same products, leading to higher profit margins and hence to higher price-sales ratios and firm value. The larger the price premium that a firm can charge, the greater is the value of the brand name. <sup>n</sup> In general, the value of a brand name can be written as: Value of brand name ={(V/S)<sup>b</sup> -(V/S)<sup>g</sup> }\* Sales (V/S)<sup>b</sup> = Value of Firm/Sales ratio with the benefit of the brand name (V/S)g = Value of Firm/Sales ratio of the firm with the generic product

## Value of Coca Cola's Brand Name

<sup>n</sup> Value of Coke's Brand Name = ( 6.10 - 0.69) (\$18,868 million) = \$102 billion <sup>n</sup> Value of Coke as a company = 6.10 (\$18,546 million) = \$ 115 Billion <sup>n</sup> Approximately 88.69% of the value of the company can be traced to brand name value
