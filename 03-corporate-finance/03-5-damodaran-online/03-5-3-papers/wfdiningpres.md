# The final decision...

## □ My rationale for rejecting the WF Dining Investment

- □ As a stand-alone investment, it does not generate a return sufficient to cover its cost of capital.

- □ Counting in the prepared foods cash flow synergy, the NPV does become positive but I remain skeptical because there may be far less expensive and resource-intensive ways of getting the increase in prepared foods sales, without opening WF Dining.

- □ At this stage of WF Dining, where there are better projects available (projects with higher NPV), this is not a good use of scarce resources (and not just capital).
