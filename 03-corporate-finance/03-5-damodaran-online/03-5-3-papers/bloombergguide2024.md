# **Guide to using Bloomberg printout**

If you have access to a Bloomberg terminal, you can print off data and information on your company in a standardized format. I use six printouts from Bloomberg, amounting to 10 pages, to get all the information I need for any company:

| Packet Page	# | Bloomberg Printout | Description                               |
|---------------|--------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| 1             | HDS                | Largest	stockholders	in	company           |
| 2             | BETA               | Beta	Regression                           |
| 3-7           | DES                | Company	description (may	be	only	4	pages) |
| 8             | CRPR               | Bond	Rating		(if	available),	Bloomberg    |
| 9             | DDIS               | Debt	Due	Distribution                     |
| 10            | FA                 | Financial	Summary	DA                      |

Alpha (Intercept): Regression intercept already in percent (0.202 is 0.202%, not 20.2%). To convert this to a Jensen's alpha = Intercept - Average Riskfree Rate (1- Raw Beta)

R^2: R-squared of regression (a number between 0 and 1), in decimals (0.275 is 27.5%)

R: Correlation between the stock and the market (a number between -1 to +1)

Std Dev of Error: Standard Deviation in difference between actual returns and expected returns, from the regression, during the period

Std Error of ALPHA: Standard error in intercept. To get a range, add or subtract one or two standard errors to or from alpha estimate

Std Error of BETA: Standard error in slope (beta) estimate. To get a range, add or subtract one or two standard errors to or from beta estimate

Ignore the rest. It is statistical debris.
