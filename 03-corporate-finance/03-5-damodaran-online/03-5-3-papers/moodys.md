## **GOVERNMENT BOND RATINGS**

I Hide Rating Definitions I

Local-Currency Government Bond Ratings reflect the government's capacity and willingness to generate local-currency revenue to repay its local-currency bonds on a timely basis.

Foreign-Currency Government Bond Ratings reflect the government's capacity and willingness to mobilize foreign exchange to repay its foreign-currency denominated bonds on a timely basis.
