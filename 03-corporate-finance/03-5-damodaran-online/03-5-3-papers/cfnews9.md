## *Miscellaneous FAQs*

*Can I change my company now?*

You can change your company anytime you want, as long as you can catch up. This is perhaps your very last chance to be able to do that.

*My company has done an acquisition or become the target of an acquisition. Am I in trouble?*

Not really. Just keep on going… The acquisition will not affect your analysis, for the moment.
