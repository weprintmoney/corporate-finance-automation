#### **This exam is worth 30% and you have 2 hours.**

1. You have been asked to value Sonata Inc., a manufacturer of musical keyboards for computers. The company has estimated its free cash flows to equity and its cost of equity for the next 4 years:

a. Estimate the terminal value of equity per share. (3 points)

b. Estimate the value per share today. ( 3 points)

The firm has return on capital of 12.5% that it expects to maintain in perpetuity.

a. Estimate the net capital expenditures for Corox, given the expected growth rate and return on capital. ( 4 points)

b. Estimate the value of the firm. (1 point)
