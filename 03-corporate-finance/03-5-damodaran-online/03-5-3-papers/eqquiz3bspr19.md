## **Quiz 3: Valuation**

Answer all questions and show necessary work. Please be brief. This is an open book, open notes exam.

- 1. You have been asked to price Colome Bank, a small privately-owned bank, planning a public offering, and have collected the following information:

|                                      | <i>Colome Bank</i> | <i>Average French Bank</i> |
|--------------------------------------|--------------------|----------------------------|
| Price to Book                        | NA                 | 1.6                        |
| Return on Equity                     | 18%                | 11%                        |
| Expected Growth Rate (in perpetuity) | 3%                 | 3%                         |

If the average bank is fairly priced, given its fundamentals, and Colome Bank is expected to have net income of \$135 million next year, estimate how much Colome's equity value will be, if fairly priced. (You can assume that Colome is as risky as the average bank.) (3 points)

- 2. Harrison Media is a young, growing media company and you are trying to price its shares today. You have been given the following information on the company:

|                            | Current    | In year 5   |
|----------------------------|------------|-------------|
| Revenue                    | \$ 300.00  | \$ 2,000.00 |
| After-tax Operating Income | \$ (30.00) | \$ 200.00   |
| Book Debt                  | \$ 200.00  | \$ 600.00   |
| Book Equity                | \$ (25.00) | \$ 800.00   |
| Cash                       | \$ 50.00   | \$ 400.00   |

The company is expected to have a cost of capital of 15% for the next 5 years, but its cost of capital will drop to 9% thereafter and its revenues will grow at 3% a year forever after that, in perpetuity; . You have run a regression of EV/Sales across media companies today and have obtained the following output:

EV/Sales = 0.50 + 12.00 (Return on Invested Capital) + 8.00 (Expected revenues growth rate in the next 5 years)

*All percentages are entered as decimals in the regression; 20% would be 0.20)*

Assuming that the company has 200 million shares outstanding today, estimate the price per share for the stock today. (You can ignore the effect of intermediate cash flows) (3 points)

- 3. You have been asked to review the valuation of a privately-owned family business for an all-equity funded transaction. If the risk free rate today is 3%, the equity risk premium is 6% and the corporate tax rate is 20%, estimate the fair value for the firm today. (4 points)
