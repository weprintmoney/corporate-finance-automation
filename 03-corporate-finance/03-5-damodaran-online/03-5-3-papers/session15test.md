## **Session 15: Post class test solutions**

- 1. To compute the cash flows, you first have to compute the depreciation each year: Depreciation=  $(150-30)/5 = 24$ . The change in working capital over the previous year also has to be computed. The salvage value will be the sum of the salvage value of the initial investment (\$30 million) and the working capital (\$30 million)

- **3. d. \$19.44 million.** The NPV should be computed using the higher cost of capital. To compute the correct NPV, we first compute the after-tax cash flow each year.
