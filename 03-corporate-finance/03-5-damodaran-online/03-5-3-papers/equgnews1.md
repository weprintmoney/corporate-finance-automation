*Data Notes…*

You can get your firm's 10-K and other Edgar filings by going into the official SEC site:

https://www.sec.gov/edgar

For macro information about interest rates, exchange rates and the rest, try the Federal

Reserve data site in St. Louis (FRED).

https://fred.stlouisfed.org

It is one on my favorites.

For a taste of equity research, you can try one of the brokerage houses and hope that they let you try out their research for free.

In fact, to see how much of their research is now outsourced, click on independent providers (the link at the bottom).
