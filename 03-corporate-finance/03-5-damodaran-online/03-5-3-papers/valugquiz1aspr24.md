## **Quiz 1: Equity Valuation**

Answer all questions and show necessary work. Please be brief. This is an open-book, open-notes exam.

1. You have been asked to assess estimate the value of an investment that Gerhard Inc, a German chemical company, is planning to make in a Turkish chemical project. You have estimated the following cash flows in millions of Turkish lira:

|                              | 1    | 2    | 3      |
|------------------------------|------|------|--------|
| Operating Income after taxes | ₺100 | ₺150 | ₺225   |
| + Depreciation               | ₺50  | ₺60  | ₺75    |
| Capital Expenditures         | ₺80  | ₺100 | ₺125   |
| FCFF                         | ₺70  | ₺110 | ₺175   |
| Terminal Value               |      |      | ₺1,508 |

Gerhard has a cost of equity of 8.50%, in Euros and for its German operations. It has no debt and is intending to follow the same policy (no debt) on its Turkish project. You have the following information on the two markets:

|               | Germany | Turkey |
|---------------|---------|--------|
| Riskfree rate | 2.50%   | 13.00% |
| ERP           | 5.00%   | 10.00% |

**Estimate the discount rate you would use to discount the Turkish lira cash flows** in the table above. (2 points)

2. Marrakesh Inc. is a Morocco-based technology company that operates in three North African countries, and the table below provides information on the three countries:

|         | Revenues (in millions of Dirham) | Currency | Government Bond Rate | Sovereign CDS |
|---------|----------------------------------|----------|----------------------|---------------|
| Egypt   | 500                              | Pound    | 21%                  | 8%            |
| Tunisia | 250                              | Dinar    | 11%                  | 4%            |
| Morocco | 750                              | Dirham   | 9%                   | 3%            |

If you assume that the equity risk premium for a mature market is 5% and that emerging market equities are 1.25 times more volatile than emerging market government bonds, **estimate the cost of equity for Marrakesh in Moroccan Dirham**. (You can assume that the beta for technology companies is 1.40) **(3 points)**

3. You are trying to estimate the beta for Stevens Media, which operates in two businesses – making movie content (studio) and subscription (streaming), and have collected the following information:

|           | Revenues (\$ million) | EV/Sales | Average Regression Beta | Average D/E |
|-----------|-----------------------|----------|-------------------------|-------------|
| Studio    | \$500                 | 1.00     | 1.100                   | 50%         |
| Streaming | \$750                 | 2.00     | 1.335                   | 15%         |

If Sevens Media has no debt, **estimate the levered beta for the company**. (The marginal tax rate is 25%) (2 points)

- 4. Rover Electric Rentals is a electric bike renting platform, built around a subscription model, and you have the following historical data on the company (in \$ millions) for the most recent years, and the three prior years.

|                          | -3          | -2    | -1      | Most Recent Year |
|--------------------------|-------------|-------|---------|------------------|
| Revenues                 | \$500       | \$750 | \$1,000 | \$1,200          |
| Customer Acquisition     | Costs \$180 | \$240 | \$300   | \$360            |
| Other Operating Expenses | \$120       | \$160 | \$200   | \$240            |
| Operating Income         | \$200       | \$350 | \$500   | \$600            |

You believe that customer acquisition costs should be capitalized, with the typical customer staying on for three years. **Estimate the corrected operating income for the most recent year, if you treat customer acquisition costs as capital expenditures.** (3 points)
