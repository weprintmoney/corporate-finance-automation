3. The company has no working capital needs but is expected to have capital expenditures of \$110 million and depreciation of \$80 million next year. - 4. An analyst (who shall remain unnamed) has valued the equity per share for Granada Inc. and concluded that it is fairly valued at its current price of \$5 a share. In reviewing the valuation, you note that:
