## **Quiz 2: Corporate Finance**

Answer all questions and show necessary work. Please be brief. This is an open books, open notes exam.

- 1. Kreider Inc. is a Swiss manufacturing company that makes watches and derives its revenues entirely in Switzerland. It is considering investing in a joint venture in Hungary to manufacture precision parts and has asked you to compute a cost of capital for the venture. You have been given the following information:
  - a. The joint venture expects to derive 60% of its revenues in Switzerland and 40% in Hungary. The equity risk premium for Hungary is 8% and the equity risk premium for Switzerland is 5%.
  - b. The unlevered beta for the watch business is 1.20 and the unlevered beta for precision parts is 0.90.
  - c. Kreider is all equity funded but the joint venture will be funded with 80% equity and 20% debt.
  - d. The joint venture is expected to be able to borrow money at a default spread of 1.5% above the risk free rate.
  - e. The risk free rate in Hungarian Forints is 6% and the risk free rate in Swiss Francs is 1%. The tax rate in Hungary is 20%.

What is the cost of capital, in Swiss Francs, for Kreider on this Hungarian joint venture?

- 2. Grover's Hardware is a hardware store, selling both large equipment (lawnmowers, snow blowers etc.) and conventional hardware. The store generated \$800,000 in after-tax operating income from revenues of \$ 10 million last year; large equipment accounted for 40% of the revenues and 75% of after-tax operating income. Grover's is considering opening a equipment servicing center on site and has the following information:

- b. The service center is expected to increase large equipment sales at the store by 20% over the current level. Assuming that revenues from equipment sales would have been flat (unchanged) for the next five years and that the after-tax operating margin on these sales would remain at the existing level for that period, estimate the effect of this "synergy" on the NPV of the project. (1.5 points)

- 3. Genzyte Inc. is a small, biotechnology firm that has just received approval from the FDA for its first drug and is considering one of two options to commercialize it:
  - a. It can license the drug to Pfizer and receive an upfront payment of \$250 million and a guaranteed (by Pfizer) licensing fee of \$200 million (pre-tax) a year for the next 15 years. At the end of year 15, Pfizer will get full rights to the drug.
- b. It can manufacture the drug itself. To do so, it has to invest \$ 1.5 billion in infrastructure, depreciable straight line to a salvage value of zero, over 15 years, to generate an EBITDA of \$650 million a year for that period. After 15 years, the sales will drop to zero. Genzyte's tax rate is 30%, its pre-tax cost of debt is 8% and its cost of capital is 12%. Pfizer's tax rate is 40%, its pre-tax cost of debt is 4% and its cost of capital is 8%. Considering risk and the time value of money, which option is the better one for Genzyte? (3 points)
