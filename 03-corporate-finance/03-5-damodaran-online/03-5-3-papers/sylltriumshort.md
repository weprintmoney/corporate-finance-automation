*Website for this class[: http://www.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New\\_Home\\_Page/cf2daydesc.html](http://www.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/cf2daydesc.html)*

## Course Objectives

*2*

¨ To give you the capacity to understand the theory and apply, in real world situations, the techniques that have been developed in corporate finance. ¤ Motto for class: If it cannot be applied, who cares?. ¨ To give you the big picture of corporate finance so that you can understand how things fit together. ¤ Motto for class: You can forget the details, but don't miss the storyline. ¨ To show you that corporate finance is fun. ¤ Motto for class: Are we having fun yet?
