## **Quiz 2: Valuation**

Answer all questions and show necessary work. Please be brief. This is an open books, open notes exam.

- 1. You have been given access the forecasted revenues, operating margin and invested capital at MobileEye Inc, a small software company that started a year ago, for the year just ended and for the next 3 years.

|                          | Last year | Year 1   | Year 2     | Year 3     |
|--------------------------|-----------|----------|------------|------------|
| Revenues                 | \$250.00  | \$600.00 | \$1,000.00 | \$1,250.00 |
| Pre-tax Operating Margin | -6.00%    | -3.00%   | 2.00%      | 5.00%      |
| Invested Capital         | \$25.00   | \$45.00  | \$60.00    | \$70.00    |

Estimate the free cash flows to the firm each year, if the firm faces a tax rate of 30% on its taxable income. (3 points)

- 2. The table below shows expected free cash flows to the firm for Fremont Food for the next 3 years, after which the company is expected to be in stable growth, growing 2% a year in perpetuity. (All dollar values in millions)

|                            | Most	recent	year | 1       | 2       | 3       |
|----------------------------|------------------|---------|---------|---------|
| Operating	Income	after	tax | \$50.00          | \$54.50 | \$59.41 | \$64.75 |
| +	Depreciation             | \$30.00          | \$32.70 | \$35.64 | \$38.85 |
| Capital	Expenditures       | \$45.00          | \$49.05 | \$53.46 | \$58.28 |
| Change	in	Working	Capital  | \$15.00          | \$16.35 | \$17.82 | \$19.43 |
| FCFF                       | \$20.00          | \$21.80 | \$23.76 | \$25.90 |

Assuming that the annual growth rate in operating income over the next three years reflects fundamentals, and that the company's return on capital is expected to remain unchanged in perpetuity, estimate the value of Fremont Food at the end of year 3 (terminal value). The cost of capital for the next three years is expected to be 10% but it will drop to 8% in stable growth. (3 points)

- 3. You are trying to value equity in Yushiko Enterprises, a company which owns 75% of Civic Lighting (its only cross holding) and you have been given both the consolidated and parent-only financial statements for Yushiko, with the key numbers summarized below (in millions):

| Expected	EBIT	(1-t) Invested	Capital | Yushiko Consolidated next	year \$150.00 \$1,500.00 | Yushiko Just	Parent \$90.00 \$1,000.00 |
|--------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| Expected	growth		(forever)           | 3%                                                 | 3%                                     |
| Cash                                 | \$150.00                                           | \$100.00                               |
| Debt                                 | \$250.00                                           | \$250.00                               |
| Minority	Interests                   | \$50.00                                            | \$-                                    |
| Cost	of	capital                      | 9%                                                 | 9%                                     |

If there are 100 million shares outstanding, estimate the value of equity per share in Yushiko Enterprises. (4 points)
