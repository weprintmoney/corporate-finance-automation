### Why your debt ratio matters...

- □ Every business, small or large, private or public, has to choose a mix of debt and equity to fund it's operations.
- □ It is possible that there exists a mix of debt and equity that is best for each business, and that the mix could be different for different businesses.
- □ If such a mix exists, we should try to find it even though we may be constrained by internal and external factors from moving to that mix.

#### *1. Bankruptcy Cost:*

Higher business risk --> Higher Cost

Greater the separation between managers and stockholders --> Greater the benefit
