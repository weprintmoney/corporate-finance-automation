## **Quiz 3: Valuation**

Answer all questions and show necessary work. Please be brief. This is an open books, open notes exam.

- 1. You have been asked to compare two publicly traded companies on EV/EBITDA multiples and want to make sure that you do it right. Each company has one minority holding (a non-controlling stake in a subsidiary) and one majority holding (a controlling stake in a different subsidiary), with the latter being consolidated. You are given the following information from the consolidated statements on the two companies:

|                                   | Suncor     | Rocco      |
|-----------------------------------|------------|------------|
| Number	of	shares	outstanding      | 600        | 400        |
| Number	of	options	outstanding     | 150        | 80         |
| Share	Price                       | \$15.00    | \$20.00    |
| Strike	Price	on	options           | \$15.00    | \$20.00    |
| Value	per	option                  | \$4.00     | \$5.00     |
| Market	value	of	Debt              | \$3,000.00 | \$2,100.00 |
| Cash	&	Marketable	Securities      | \$1,000.00 | \$500.00   |
| Minority	Holdings	(Market	Value)  | \$800.00   | \$2,500.00 |
| Minority	Interests	(Market	Value) | \$1,200.00 | \$1,500.00 |
| EBITDA                            | \$1,200.00 | \$900.00   |

Estimate which of these companies is cheaper on an EV/EBITDA basis. (2 points)

- 2. ScandLook Furniture is a premium brand furniture company, which charges high prices for furniture and settles for low volume. Next year, it expects to generate \$30 million in after-tax operating income on \$150 million in revenues and it currently trades at an EV/Sales ratio of 3.00. If generic furniture manufacturers have the same sales to invested capital ratio as Scandlook but earn half its operating margin (in aftertax terms), estimate the value of Scandlook's brand name, assuming that Scandlook is fairly priced by the market. (All furniture companies are in stable growth, growing 3% a year and have a cost of capital of 9%). (3 points)

- 3. Large money-center banks are trading at about 87.5% of book value (Price to Book = 0.8) and collectively report a return on equity of 9% and are expected to grow earnings 2% a year forever. If you believe that the low pricing is fair and is because investors perceive banks to be riskier than they have been historically, estimate the (levered) beta for banks. (The risk free rate is 2% and the equity risk premium is 6%) (2 points)

- 4. You are valuing a small privately-owned plumbing business for sale to an individual (a plumber who plans to invest all his money in it). The business is all equity funded, is expected to generate \$200,000 in after-tax operating income next year, grow 3% a year in perpetuity and generate a return on invested capital of 20% on new investments. If publicly traded companies in this business have an unlevered beta of 0.8 and the correlation of these companies with the market is 40%, estimate the value of the plumbing business (for this transaction). The risk free rate is 2% and the equity risk premium is 6%. (3 points)
