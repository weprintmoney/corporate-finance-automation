(7) If the new debt or equity is used to take projects, the analysis would change for two reasons –

- (a) the projects may have a different risk profile than the firm's risk profile.
- (b) the NPV of the projects has to be added to the value change calculated.
- (c) the firm value itself will increase as the new debt and equity is issued.

(8) I would factor in the firm's need for flexibility into the analysis - the greater the need for flexibility the less likely it is that I would add on debt. Further, I would look at how responsive managers are to stockholders; if they are not, I would be more likely to add debt.

(9) The higher rating in option 1 lowers the cost of debt, but it is accomplished by replacing debt with more expensive equity.
