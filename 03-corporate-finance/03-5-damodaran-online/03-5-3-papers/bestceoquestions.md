 week,

 one

 quality

 a 
 company's

 the

 return

 invested 
 capital

 its

 capital.

 well 
 managed

 high

 excess 
 returns

 firms

 not,

 raw

 management

 little 
 to 
 earn.

That 
 is 
 partly 
 why 
 I 
 think 
 that 
 this 
 listing 
 of 
 the 
 world's 
 best 
 CEOs 
 is 
 interesting 
 and 
 debatable. This 
 list 
 is 
 based 
 primarily 
 on 
 shareholder 
 returns 
 and 
 here

 some 
 questions

- 1. What 
  are 
  the 
  determinants 
  of 
  shareholder 
  returns?

  that 
  you 
  can 
  ask 
   the

   question 
   about 
   Jensen's 
   alphas)

  much

  excess

  projects

   shareholder 
   returns?

   you

   that 
   companies 
   that

   capital,

   cost 
   of 
   capital,

   returns

   stockholders?)

  much

  the 
  success

  the 
  highlighted

  you
