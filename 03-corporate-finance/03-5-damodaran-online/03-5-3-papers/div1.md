| There may be several factors behind the positive reaction to spin offs. First, the spun off division may be worth more as an independent entity than as part of a larger company (a reverse synergy argument). Second, the spun off division may be freer to pursue plans it could not pursue as part of the larger entity, either because of regulatory concerns or corporate culture. Finally, the very act of the spin off may force analysts to look at the value of the spun off entity and realize that they have been undervaluing it. |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |
