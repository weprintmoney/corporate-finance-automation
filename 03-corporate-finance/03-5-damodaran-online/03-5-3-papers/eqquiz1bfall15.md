## **Quiz 1: Valuation**

Answer all questions and show necessary work. Please be brief. This is an open books, open notes exam.

- 1. Wishman Corporation is a US-based steel company with a wholly owned mining subsidiary in Peru In the most recent year, the company reported the following statistics for its US and Peruvian operations.

|      |        |      | Revenue      | Cash                   | Expected       | Cost           | of      |           |  |
|------|--------|------|--------------|------------------------|----------------|----------------|---------|-----------|--|
|      |        |      | (in US \$    | (in US \$              | Growth         | Cost of Equity | Capital | (in US \$ |  |
|      |        |      | (in US \$ \$ | (in US \$ \$           | Rate (in US \$ | (in US \$      | local   |           |  |
|      |        |      | millions)    | millions) <sup>a</sup> | \$             | currency       | local   | currency  |  |
| US   | Steel  | 1000 | 100          | 1.00%                  | 9.00%          | (US\$)         | 7.50%   | (US\$)    |  |
| Peru | Mining | 500  | 75           | 2.00%                  | 14.00%         | (Sul)          | 12.50%  | (Sul)     |  |

*<sup>a</sup> Cash flow computed after taxes & reinvestment, but before debt payments.*

Wishman is planning to sell its Peruvian subsidiary. If the inflation rate in Peruvian Sul is 6% and the inflation rate in US\$ is 1.5%, what value would you attach to it, assuming that the growth rates hold in perpetuity? (2.5 points)

- 2. Werner Inc. is a Germany-based company with operations also in Poland and Croatia with the following operating details:

|         | Revenues (in millions Euros) | Govt Bond rate in Euros | Govt Bond rate in local currency | Relative Equity Market Volatility |
|---------|------------------------------|-------------------------|----------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| Germany | € 500.00                     | 1.00%                   | 1.00% (Euros)                    | NA                                |
| Poland  | € 300.00                     | 2.50%                   | 4.00% (Zloty)                    | 2.00                              |
| Croatia | € 200.00                     | 5.00%                   | 9.00% (Kuna)                     | 1.50                              |

*b Relative Equity Market Volatility = Std Dev of Local Equity Index/ Std Dev of Local Govt Bond*

Estimate the cost of equity for Werner Inc. in Euros, assuming that the levered beta for the company is 1.20. (You can assume that the equity risk premium for mature markets is 6% and that Germany is a mature market.) (2.5 points)

- 3. Wholesome Corp is a food processing company that also operates restaurants. In the most recent year, the company reported the following for its businesses:

| Business        | Revenues (in	\$ millions) | EV/Sales | Unlevered	Beta |
|-----------------|---------------------------|----------|----------------|
| Food	Processing | \$400.00                  | 1.5      | 0.9            |
| Restaurants     | \$600.00                  | 0.5      | 0.6            |

If the marginal tax rate for the company is 40%, the company has \$300 million in debt outstanding and the company is trading at a fair value, relative to its peers, estimate the levered beta for the company. (You can assume that the company has a negligible cash balance.) (2.5 points)
