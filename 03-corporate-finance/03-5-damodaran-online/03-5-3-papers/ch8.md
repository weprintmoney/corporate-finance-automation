# CASH RETURN: MEASURING POTENTIAL DIVIDENDS

Start with equity earnings

Net Income

*plus*

Add back non-cash expenses

Depreciation & Amortization

*minus*

Capital Expenditures & Acquisitions

*minus*

Net out capital expenditures

Change in non-cash Working Capital

Net out working capital needs

*plus*

Add (subtract) debt issued (repaid)

(New Borrowings - Debt repaid)

*equals*

Gives you cash that can be returned

**Free Cash Flow to Equity (Potential Dividend)**

Reinvestment for future growth

# A GLOBAL PICTURE OF CASH RETURN VS FCFE

Returning no cash when a firm has positive FCFE or returning less cash than is available, as FCFE, adds to cash balances.

Returning cash when a firm has negative FCFE or returning more cash than is available as FCFE will burn through cash.
