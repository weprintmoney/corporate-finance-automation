## **Quiz 1: Valuation**

Answer all questions and show necessary work. Please be brief. This is an open books, open notes exam.

- 1. Scully Corporation is a US-based steel company with a wholly owned mining subsidiary in Brazil. In the most recent year, the company reported the following numbers for its US and Brazilian operations.

| Country | <i>Business</i> | <i>Revenue (in US \$ millions)</i> | <i>Cash flow (in US \$ millions)<sup>a</sup></i> | <i>Expected Growth Rate (in US \$)</i> | <i>Cost of Equity (in local currency)</i> | <i>Cost of Capital (in local currency)</i> |
|---------|-----------------|------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| US      | Steel           | \$1000                             | \$ 100                                           | 1.00%                                  | 9.00% (US \$)                             | 7.50% (US \$)                              |
| Brazil  | Mining          | \$1000                             | \$ 150                                           | 2.00%                                  | 21.00% (\$R)                              | 17.50% (\$R)                               |

*<sup>a</sup> Cash flow is computed after taxes & reinvestment, but before debt payments.*

Scully is planning to sell its Brazilian subsidiary. If the inflation rate in \$R is 9% and the inflation rate in US\$ is 1.5%, what value would you attach to it, assuming that the growth rates hold in perpetuity? (2.5 points)

- 2. Schroeder Inc. is a Germany-based company with operations also in Poland and Hungary, with the following operating details:

| Country | Revenues	(in millions	of Euros) | Govt	Bond	rate in	millions	of Euros | Govt	Bond	rate	in local	currency | Relative	Equity Market Volatilityb |
|---------|---------------------------------|-------------------------------------|----------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| Germany | 900                             | 1.00%                               | 1.00%                            | NA                                 |
| Poland  | 300                             | 2.50%                               | 4.00% (Zlotys)                   | 2.00                               |
| Hungary | 800                             | 4.00%                               | 7.50% (Forint)                   | 1.50                               |

*b Relative Equity Market Volatility = Std Dev of Local Equity Index/ Std Dev of Local Govt Bond*

Estimate the cost of equity for Schroeder Inc. in Euros, assuming that the levered beta for the company is 1.20. (You can assume that the equity risk premium for mature markets is 6% and that Germany is a mature market.) (2.5 points)

- 3. Good Foods Corp is a food processing company that also operates grocery stores. In the most recent year, the company reported the following for its businesses:

| <i>Comparable Companies</i> |                           |                        |                |     |
|-----------------------------|---------------------------|------------------------|----------------|-----|
| <i>Business</i>             | Revenues (in \$ millions) | Enterprise Value/Sales | Unlevered Beta | D/E |
| Food Processing             | 800                       | 1.5                    | 0.9            | 25% |
| Grocery Stores              | 1200                      | 0.5                    | 0.6            | 60% |

If the marginal tax rate for the company is 40%, the company has \$900 million in debt outstanding and the company is trading at a fair value, relative to its peers, estimate the levered beta for the company. (You can assume that the company has a negligible cash balance.) (2.5 points)

- 4. You have been given the income statement and the statement of cash flows for Reinold Enterprises for the most recent year (in \$ millions):

| Income	Statement                            |          | Statement	of	Cash	Flows |           |
|---------------------------------------------|----------|-------------------------|-----------|
| Revenues                                    | \$500.00 | Operating CF            |           |
| Operating	Expenses	(including	Depreciation) | \$300.00 | Net	Income              | \$105.00  |
| Operating	Income                            | \$200.00 | Depreciation            | \$80.00   |
| Interest	expenses                           | \$50.00  | Change	in	non-cash	WC   | -\$20.00  |
| Taxable	Income                              | \$150.00 | Investing CF            |           |
| Taxes                                       | \$45.00  | Cap	Ex                  | -\$100.00 |
| Net	Income                                  | \$105.00 | Acquisitions            | -\$60.00  |
|                                             |          | Financing CF            |           |
|                                             |          | Net	New	Debt            | \$40.00   |
|                                             |          | Dividends               | -\$50.00  |
|                                             |          | Change	in	cash	balance  | -\$5.00   |

*In statement of cash flows, the sign indicates whether it is a positive or a negative cash flow.*

Estimate the free cash flow to the firm in the most recent year. (You can assume that the effective tax rate = marginal tax rate). (2.5 points)
