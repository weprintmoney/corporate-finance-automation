Sheets: Variables & FAQ, Industry Averages

## Variables & FAQ

| End Game | To estimate ratios of market values (equity and firm) to book value (equity and firm), with companion variables (ROE and ROC) |
|---|---|
|  |  |
| Variable | Explanation |
| Number of firms | Number of firms in the industry grouping. |
| PS | Aggregated market capitalization divided by aggregated revenues, across all firms in the group. |
| Net Margin | Aggregate net income divided by aggregated revenues, across companies in group. |
| EV/ Sales | Aggregated enterprise value divided by aggregated revenues across all firms in the group. (EV = Market Cap + Debt - Cash) |
| Pre-tax Operating Margin | Aggregate after-tax operating income divided by aggregated in revenues, across companies in group. |
