# Ch6 15

Source: http://www.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pc/acf4E/ch6-15.xls

Sheets: Sheet1

## Sheet1

| Company | Competitive Advantage(s) | Sustainability | Challenges |
|---|---|---|---|
| Disney | Disney has core advantages that are almost impossible for other firms to replicate and the firm has done best when it has focused on those businesses where it can use these strengths. Using this template, the acquisition of Pixar and even the investment in the cruise line business (which uses Disney characters to appeal to families) make sense. Disney has faltered when it has strayed from this core mission, as was the case with its early investments in the internet business, sports teams (California Angels) and even conventional broadcasting (ABC). Disney’s crown jewel, ironically, today is the ESPN franchise that came out of the Capital Cities acquisition, generating huge cash flows for the company from its preeminent position as the “sports” cable network. | Disney's strength is content. It owns iconic brand names in entertainment and has learned how to use them well. | Technology is changing the business and may affect Disney's capacity to profit on content |
| Vale | Vale’s key advantage is its control of vast quantities of iron ore and other commodity reserves globally. | Natural resources are limited, thus providing a long term competitive advantage. | Vale is depended on economic growth, especially in China for its success. |
| Tata Motors | Tata Motors has the advantage of being an established automobile manufacturer, with an unmatched distribution system in India. The acquisition of Landrover and Jaguar have also given Tata Motors two preeminent brand names and have allowed it to grow its revenues and profits, especially in China. | Cost advantages have faded as other auto makers have moved into India. The Landrover & Jaguar brand names are likely to survive. | Company will be much more affected by economic cycles, as more of its revenues shift to luxury brands. |
| Deutsche Bank | As a large bank with global resources and multiple business lines, Deutsche may be able to act where its competitors cannot act. | Depends on how other large banks react to last banking crisis as well as regulatory moves. | Large banks may be forced out of their most profitable businesses because of fears of systemic risk affected banking. |
| Baidu | It's  dominance in China, the largest, fastest growing online market in the world. While its search engine may not be unique or particularly creative, it is attuned to Chinese users, giving Baidu a significant competitive advantage over Google and Yahoo! | If the Chinese government continues to favor Baidu over Google, that will help Baidu at least in the near term. | If the Chinese government opens up the business to competition, Baidu may not be able to match up to foreign competitors. |
