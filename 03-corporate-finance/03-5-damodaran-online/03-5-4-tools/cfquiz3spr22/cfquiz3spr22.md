## Quiz 3a

| Problem 1 |
|---|
| Corbin Inc. currently has $900 million in market cap and $100 million in debt, and at the company's current debt ratio, the cost of equity is 7.90% and the pre-tax cost of borrowing is 4%. The company is considering doubling its debt to capital ratio and if it does so, you expect the default spread on debt to also double. If the risk free rate is 3%, the equity risk premium is 5% and the marginal tax rate is 20%, estimate the cost of capital at the new debt ratio. (4 points) |
| Problem 2 |
| Rankin Corp. is an all-equity funded publicly traded company that is considering a large stock buyback. It has 80 million shares outstanding, trading at $25 a share, with a cost of equity of 9%. By borrowing $600 million and buying back shares at $30/share, the company expects to lower its cost of capital to 8%. Assuming it goes through with the buyback, estimate the value per share, of the remaining shares, after the buyback. (You can assume that the savings are perpetual and have no growth) (3 points) |
| Problem 3 |
| Fantex Inc. is a company in the retail business, with a total enterprise value of $1.2 billion and $200 million in debt (with a duration of 2 years). The company is considering acquiring a construction company for $800 million in debt, using a mix of 2-year zero coupons and 20-year zero coupons. If the duration of the retail business is 5 years and the duration of the construction business is 12.5 years, what percent of the new debt issue should be in 20-year notes for the duration of the debt = duration of the assets? (3 points) |
