# Histgreurope24

Source: https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pc/archives/histgrEurope24.xls

Sheets: Variables & FAQ, Industry Averages

## Variables & FAQ

| End Game | To report historical and expected growth rates in operating metrics. The historical growth rates are compounded, annual values (CAGR). |
|---|---|
|  |  |
| Variable | Explanation |
| Number of firms | Number of firms in the industry grouping. |
| CAGR in Net Income (Last 5 years) | Average of compounded annual growth rate in net income over last 5 years, among firms that have been in existence > 5 years. Only includes firms with positive earnings five years ago and current year. |
| CAGR in Revenues (Last 5 years) | Average of compounded annual growth rate in revenues over last 5 years, among firms that have been in existence > 5 years. |
| Expected Growth in Revenues - Next 2 years | Average of analyst estimates of annual growth rate in revenues for next two years. |
| Expected Growth in EPS - Next 5 years | Average of analyst estimates of annual growth rate in earnings  per sahre for next five years. |
