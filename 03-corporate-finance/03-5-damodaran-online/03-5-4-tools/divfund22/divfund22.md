## Variables & FAQ

| End Game | Fundamental determinants of dividend policy (and payment) |
|---|---|
|  |  |
| Variable | Explanation |
| Number of firms | Number of firms in the indusry grouping. |
| Total Dividends | Aggregated dividends paid by companies in the group, including both regular and special dividends. |
| Special Dividends as % of Total Dividends | Aggreagted value of special dividends/ Aggregated value of total dividends |
| Dividend Payout | Aggregated Total Dividends/ Aggregated Net Income |
| Dividend Yield | Aggregated Total Dividends/ Aggregated Market Capitalization of Equity |
| Market Cap | Aggregated value of market values of equity of firms in the industry grouping. |
| ROE (Return on equity) | Aggregated Net Income/ Aggregated Book Value of Equity |
| Institutional Holding | Proportion of outstanding shares in company, held by institutiions (mutual funds, pension funds etc.), averaged across companies in the group. |
| Standard Deviation of Equity | Annualized standard deviation in stock returns, averaged across firms in group. |
