| Notes: |
| Actual As Reported Diluted earnings, sometimes called Generally Accepted Accounting Principal (GAAP) earnings, is income from continuing operations. It excludes both |
| discontinued and extraordinary income. Both of these terms are defined by Financial Standards Accounting Board (FASB) under GAAP. As Reported earnings represent the |
| longest monitored earnings series available today. |
| Actual Operating Basic earnings then excludes 'unusual' items from that value. Operating income is not defined under GAAP by FASB. This permits individual companies to |
| interpret what is and what is not 'unusual'. The result is a varied interpretation of items and charges, where the same specific type of charge may be included in Operating earnings |
| for one company and omitted from another. S&P reviews all earnings to insure compatibility across company and industry groups. |
|  |
| The bottom-up earnings estimates are based on the Capital IQ consensus forecast. |
| The current As Reported reporting quarter consists of actually-reported earnings extrapolated if at least 70% of the market value has reported. |
| The current Operating reporting quarter consists of actually-reported earnings and Capital IQ consensus estimates. |
| Historical annual P/Es are based on the year-end price. |
| Estimated P/Es are based on the current price. |

## COMMENTARY AND BEATS

| Howard Silverblatt, S&P Dow Jones Indices Senior Index Analyst | Upcoming: December 13, 2013 - December 20, 2013 |
|---|---|
|  | Earnings will pick up next week, as off fiscals start to report the fourth quarter. Earnings will start after the close of Tuesday’s market with database management software maker, Oracle (ORCL), which is expected to post a 5% gain year-over-year earnings.  Wednesday morning will bring earnings from packaging delivery issue, FedEx (FDX), which is expected to increase 9%, as investors look to the company’s analysis of shipping for a general evaluation of the economy.  Consumer foods and restaurant issue, General Mills (GIS) is expected to post a modest 2% gain. After the close, circuit board and assemblies’ issue, Jabil Circuit (JBL) is expected to post an 11% decline, as computer payroll and accounting services issue, Paychex (PAYX) is expected to post a 2% gain.  Thursday will open with management and consulting services issue, Accenture (ACN) expected to see a 4% gain in earnings, as the operator of family style restaurants, Darden Restaurants (DRI) is expected to post a 15% decline. After the close will be sales and rental of uniforms issue, Cintas (CTAS), expected to post an 8% gain.  Athletic footwear issue, NIKE (NKE) will become the first Dow Jones Industrial Average 30 issue to report its earnings, and is expected to post a slight increase in earnings.  Computer software and services issue, Red Hat (RHT) is expected to post a 21% gain.  Friday will bring new and used auto retail auto seller, Carmax (KMX), expected to post a 17% gain in earnings, as retail drug chain store, Walgreen (WAG) is expected to post a 29% gain. Economic reports will start Monday with the third quarter update on Productivity, which is expected to increase to 2.2% from the prior 1.9% release, as Unit Labor cost is expected to be reduced by 1.0% from the prior 0.6% reduction. Industrial Production for November is expected to come in 0.3% higher, after October’s 0.1% declined. Capacity Utilization for November is expected to have increased to 78.3% from October’s 78.1%  Tuesday will bring November’s CPI, expected to be up 0.1% for the month, as the year-over-year reading is expected to be up 1.3%.  Ex food and energy is expected to be up 0.1% for November, and up 1.7% year-over-year. Wednesday will bring the weekly new mortgage application report.  Housing starts for October is expected to have increased to the 941,000 annual rate, with November is expected to be 918,000.  Permits are expected to have come in at the 1 million level for November.  Home completion for October is expected to have been at the 825,000 annual level, as November is expected to be at the 855,000 level. Thursday will start with the new weekly unemployment claims report.  Leading Indicators for November are expected to increase 0.5%, as existing home sales for November are expected to come in at the 5,055,000 annual level.  On Friday, third quarter GDP will be updated, and is expected to tick down to 3.5% from the previous 3.6%.   |
