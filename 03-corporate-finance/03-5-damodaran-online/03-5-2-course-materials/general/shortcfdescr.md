#### The Project

* [Project
  Description](https://www.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pdfiles/execs/triumproj.pdf). Describes what the project is about and the questions
  that need to be answered.
* [Number
  Checking](https://www.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pc/datasets/summercos2005.xls): You can check to see whether you are hopelessly
  off on your numbers (and why) by going to this spreadsheet
  which contains my computed numbers for each of your companies.
* [FAQ](cfprojectsumm.htm): These are some of the issues that have turned up in prior
  iterations. Check to see if your question is answered here.
* [Sample Project Write-Up](Seminars/disneySh.htm): My
  project write up for Disney. Use it as a rough guide but not
  as the only or even the best template for your own write up.

For examples from the applied corporate finance book as well as solutions, try the supporting site.
