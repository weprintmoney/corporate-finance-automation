include the webcasts from the current class. The webcasts can be accessed in
one of four ways:

1. Streaming video (The file will be streamed to your computer. This will work only
   if you have a good internet connection, but should be of the best quality)
2. Video podcast (The .m4v file will be downloaded to your computer and you can
