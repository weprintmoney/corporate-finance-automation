I do teach shorter-term executive classes, ranging from two to three days, at the Stern School of Business and mostly for the Trium Executive MBA program. These classes are challenging not so much because you have less teaching time (you do, but not much less) but for two other reasons: (a) the material piles on top of itself, making it difficult to assimilate, especially if you are new to finance and (b) you don't get to try stuff out on your own, screw up, and try again.
