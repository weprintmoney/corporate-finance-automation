**Valuation of the Week
#4: Shake Shack**

As you are undoubtedly aware, Shake Shack made a splash with
its IPO a few weeks ago, when it opened at $50/share, well above its offering
price of $21. Before you start looking at the number, you should start by
sampling the company's products at Madison Park (but be prepared to wait in
line). The company's story is told at this link:

<https://www.shakeshack.com>

Once you have the story, you should read the prospectus
filed by the company. It is instructive in the detail it provides on how the
founders are tilting the scales in their favor. For a laugh, read the risk
section, where the company lists everything that can go wrong:

<http://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1620533/000104746915000523/a2222881z424b3.htm>

The next story is about the pricing of the IPO at $21/share,
with some perceiving that as too high a number:

<http://www.wsj.com/articles/shake-shack-prices-ipo-at-21-above-expectations-1422571165>

On the opening day, the market proved them all wrong by
popping up more than 100%:

<http://www.npr.org/blogs/thesalt/2015/01/30/382658859/shake-shack-sizzles-with-ipo-as-mcdonald-s-fizzles>

Before you start entering numbers into your valuation of the
company, get a measure of the landscape by downloading the data on all publicly
traded restaurants in the United States. Get a measure of what the big players
generate as revenues and what they earn as margins. The dataset also lists the
number of restaurants that each company has and you can compare it to the 63
that Shake Shack has open.

<http://www.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pc/blog/restaurants.xls>

The dataset also has multiples computed (PE, PBV, EV/Sales
etc.) and I have also computed the enterprise value per restaurant, for what it
is worth.

Now, you can try the spreadsheet to value Shake Shack. I
have the basic numbers already entered into the spreadsheet. The numbers that
you can try tweaking are revenue growth rates and operating margins. As you
play with the revenue growth, check your revenues in year 10 and compare them
to the revenues of other restaurants. In other words, are you shooting for
Starbucks, Chipotle or The Cheesecake Factory)?  (I shot for Ruby Tuesday..) I already have given Shake Shack the operating margin of
the 75th percentile of restaurants but you are welcome to shoot even
higher.

<http://www.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pc/blog/ShakeShack2015.xls>

When you are done with your valuation, please go into the
shared Google spreadsheet and enter your numbers.

<https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1rCy3PJeddXgMqNKrvh4ZElncpDp7-qzF6bOVafGlSK0/edit?usp=sharing>
