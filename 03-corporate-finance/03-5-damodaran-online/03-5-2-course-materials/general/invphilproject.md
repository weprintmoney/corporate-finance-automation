**Investment Philosophies Project**

               The
ultimate grading for this course will rest on a prospectus that each group will
prepare. In the prospectus, you will lay out your investment philosophy and how
you plan to put it into practice. In particular, as a potential investor in the
fund that you are starting, I will be looking for the following �

if !supportLists1.       endifWhat
is your investment philosophy and how is it related to your view of how markets
work?

if !supportLists2.      endifHow
do you propose to convert this investment philosophy into an investment strategy
or strategies?

if !supportLists3.      endifWhat
specific constraints would you put on yourself? For instance, are there certain
types of investors you would want investing in fund and certain types of
investors you would dissuade from investing? Is there a minimum size for your
fund to be viable? Is there a maximum size beyond which your fund will no
longer be viable?

if !supportLists4.      endifIs
there any evidence you can point to, either that you have done or others have
done, that would suggest that your investment strategy will work?

if !supportLists5.      endifHow
would you go about back testing this strategy? (Set up the test even if you
cannot get your hands on the data. If you can, you will get extra credit fro
actual back testing)

if !supportLists6.      endifHow
would you propose that you should be evaluated? (How often? On what yardstick?)

if !supportLists7.      endifBased
upon your investment strategy, develop a portfolio of at least 25 stocks that
you would hold right now.
