### The Power of the Story

Investment stories have been around for as long as you have
had financial markets and they show remarkable longevity. The same stories are
recycled with each generation of investors and presented as new and different
by their proponents. The stories that will be examined in the chapters to come
have been laid out and categorized by the human emotion that makes each one so
compelling;  some stories appeal to
the fearful (risk averse), others the hopeful and still other to the greedy. The
process that will be used in each chapter to examine each of the investment
stories is also laid out, starting with the story, followed by the theoretical
foundations and the evidence on its effectiveness (or lack thereof) and closing
with its potential weaknesses (and ways of protecting yourself against them).
