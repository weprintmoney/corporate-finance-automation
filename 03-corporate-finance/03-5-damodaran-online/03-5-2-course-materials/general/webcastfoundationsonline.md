This is a course of 12 short webcasts (about 12-20 minutes
apiece), designed to introduce you to the basics of finance.  It is meant to provide the foundtatons that I build on in my corporate finance , valuation  and investment philosophy classes. With each session, you can download
slides for that session and a post-class test to go with it (and solutions). If you have my book, the relevant sections of the book are highlighted. The class webcasts are on You Tube and you will need to be online, to watch them.
