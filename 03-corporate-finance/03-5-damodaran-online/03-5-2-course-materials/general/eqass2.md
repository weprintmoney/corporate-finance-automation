I don't impose a specific template on the projects but I know that you are looking for some guidance. To help (or perhaps not), I have included not only sample projects from prior years that I think did the job but also provided summaries of valuations from prior semesters (going back to 2000). I have never been able to eke out big lessons out of them, but you are welcome to try.

***Sample Projects***

This project is free form and I accept a number of different formats,
as long as they convey the basic content - valuations and recommendations.Here
are a few examples.
