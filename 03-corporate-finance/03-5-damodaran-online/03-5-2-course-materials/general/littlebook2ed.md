This web site is designed to support "The Little Book of Valuation", the revised edition (published in 2024). This edition updates the 2012 version, bringing in new companies to illustrate the challenges of valuation at different stages of the life cycle, and in different sectors. The publisher is John Wiley and Sons. Since it is one of many books that
I have on valuation, it behooves me to explain how this book is different
from my rest and I [attempt to do so here](littlebook/preface.htm). Once you have got that out of the
way, you can get a [quick review of the tools](#tools) that make up the valuation toolkit and
then move on to [valuation inputs and mechanics.](#inputs) The next two sections look
at adapting valuation models for different typies of companies - [across
the life cycle](#lifecycle) and then [across sectors](#sectors). The [last
section](#addons) has a glossary and links
to datasets and spreadsheets. Along the way, I also provide links to the annual reports and filings that I used in the valuations to allow you to recreate the numbers.

YouTube Playlist for the book: <https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLUkh9m2Borql51x9Lj8S-bHUq3V6kuix->

Unlike the pope, I am extremely fallible, and with a book that is as full of numbers as this one, there are errors that creep in. The corrections that I have found so far are at this link:  
Book corrections: [https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar//New\_Home\_Page/littlebook2/LBV2corrections.html](littlebook2/LBV2corrections.html)
