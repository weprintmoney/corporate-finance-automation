***Webcasts***  
This page will include the webcasts from the current class. This page will
include the webcasts from the current class. The webcasts can be accessed in
one of four ways:

1. Streaming video (The file will be streamed to your computer. This will work only
   if you have a good internet connection, but should be of the best quality)
2. Video podcast (The .m4v file will be downloaded to your computer and you can
   watch the video using Quicktime or video software or on your iPod)
3. Audio podcast (The .mp3 file will be downloaded as an audio file and can be played
   on any audio player)
4. YouTube videos (if you have slower internet connection and/or a small device).
