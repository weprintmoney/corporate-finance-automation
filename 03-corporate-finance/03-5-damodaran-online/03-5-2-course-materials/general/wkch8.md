Weekly Challenge 8

stock, trading at $ 28 today, through a variety of analysis. The summary
results are presented below:

Approach
used                    Value
per share

DCF                                    $
22

Relative
valuation

PE
Ratio (comparables)      $
35: Compared to other companies in the sector

PE
Ratio (market)               $
28: Compared to other companies in the market

1. How would you describe your results (and
   resolve the inconsistent conclusions) to someone who is a novice at
   valuation? In other words, how would you explain how a stock can be under,
   over and correctly valued at the same time?
2. Assuming that all of your valuations are
   right, can you estimate whether and by how much the sector is misvalued?
