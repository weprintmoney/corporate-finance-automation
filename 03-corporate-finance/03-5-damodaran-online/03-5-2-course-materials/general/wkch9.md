Weekly Challenge 10

You have been
asked to value a technology patent for a telecomm firm and have come up with
the following inputs into the valuation:

if !supportListsq     
endifIf you introduced the patent today, the present value
of your expected cashflows would be $ 1.025 billion.

if !supportListsq     
endifThe cost of the introduction is expected to be $ 1
billion today.

if !supportListsq     
endifYou have the patent for the next 16 years

if !supportListsq     
endifThe standard deviation in firm value of publicly traded
research-oriented telecomm firms is 50%

if !supportListsq     
endifThe riskless rate is 5%

if !supportListsa.     endifEstimate
the value of the patent as an option.

if !supportListsb.     endifEstimate
the net present value of converting the patent into a commercial product today.

if !supportListsc.     endifWhat
do the answers to the first two questions tell you about whether you should
convert the patent into a product today?

if !supportListsd.     endifHolding
the present value of the cashflows and the cost of introduction fixed � I know
that this is unrealistic � estimate the year in which it would be optimal to
convert the patent.

if !supportListse.     endifHow
would your answers to the previous questions change if you knew that a
competitor was 7 years away from developing an equivalent product?
