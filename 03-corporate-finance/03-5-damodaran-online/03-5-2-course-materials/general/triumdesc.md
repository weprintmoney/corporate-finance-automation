This is a compressed corporate finance class, but I will try to cover
the entire subject matter in 3 days. I know that this sounds intimidating,
especially if you have never taken a corporate finance class before.
However, trust me, it is doable. In fact, [read
this](triumintro.html) before you go
on.

#### Before you begin

* [Syllabus for the class](https://www.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pdfiles/execs/syllTriumShort.pdf)

  feel you need to get up to speed)

#### The Project (For more, [try this link](triumproj.html))

* [Bloomberg packet](https://www.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pdfiles/BloombergGuide.pdf): If your data comes from a Bloomberg packet, this link will provide descriptions on what is in it, and how it can be used for the project.
* [Project
  Description](https://www.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pdfiles/execs/triumproj.pdf). Describes what the project is about and the questions
  that need to be answered.
* [Project Workbook](https://www.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pdfiles/execs/projworkbookJan2019.pdf): This is your handy, must-not-miss, indispensable guide to analyzing your company. (Will be ready a week before class)
* [Number
  Checking](https://www.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pc/numberchecker2019.xlsx): You can check to see whether you are hopelessly
  off on your numbers (and why) by going to this spreadsheet.
* [FAQ](cfprojectsumm.htm): These are some of the issues that have turned up in prior
  iterations. Check to see if your question is answered here.
* [Sample Project Write-Ups](triumpastprojects.html): These are sample projects from prior years. As you will see, there is no fixed format but the end game is the same.
