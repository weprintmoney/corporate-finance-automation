Corporate finance is (or should be) at the core of business education
everywhere(and I am not being biased). It is the true big picture
class, drawing on accounting, statistics, economics and every management
discipline to develop the first principles that should govern decision
making within any business, small and large, public and private and
across all markets.
I should not be playing favorites, but I love teaching this class and
I hope it shows. This is the entry page for my semester-long corporate finance class, composed of 26 sessions. You can find almost everything you need in this page, either in the pull down menu above or in the menu below.
