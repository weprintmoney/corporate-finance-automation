This is a course of 25 short webcasts (about 12-20 minutes
apiece), designed both to capture what I do in my regular semester-long
valuation class and to supplement my books on valuation. With each session, you can download
slides for that session and a post-class test to go with it (and solutions). If you have my book, the relevant sections of the book are highlighted. The first part are the webcast related to the class and the second part are tools webcasts, designed to help you apply the concepts to real companies. The class webcasts are on YouTube and you will need to be online, to watch them. The in-practice webcasts are downloadable to your computer or device and can be watched at your convenience. I have also created a version of this class on iTunes U, and you can get to that class by [clicking here](https://itunes.apple.com/us/course/id780803998).
