*Week 2: Estimating a riskfree rate and country risk premium*

           
You are trying to estimate a risk free rate in Peruvian Sol and the country
risk premium that you would apply to Peru. You have collected the following
information:

*Government bond rates
(in local currency) by country*

*CDS spreads by country*

if !vml![](wkch2new_files/image004.png)endif

In addition, you have estimated the standard deviation in
the Peruvian equity index over the last two years to be 28% and the standard
deviation in the Peruvian government bond over the same period at 18%.

The U.S. treasury
bond rate is 2%, the historical risk premium of stocks over T.bonds
over the last 83 years in the U.S. has been 4.10% and the annualized standard
deviation in U.S. equities has been 20%.
