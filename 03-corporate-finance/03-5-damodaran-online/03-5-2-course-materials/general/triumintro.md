This is a compressed corporate finance class, but I will try to cover
the entire subject matter in a short period (2-3 days). I know that this sounds intimidating,
especially if you have never taken a corporate finance class before.
However, trust me, it is doable.

#### Before you begin

* [Syllabus for the class](https://www.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pdfiles/execs/triumsyllabus.pdf)
* Pre-reading on tools you need for finance (if you
  feel you need to get up to speed)
  + [Time Value of Money (or Do you really trust your financial calculator?)](PVPrimer/pvprimer.htm)
  + [Accounting (Boring, but necessary.. I guess)](AccPrimer/accstate.htm)

#### The Project

* [Project
  Description](https://www.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pdfiles/execs/triumproj.pdf). Describes what the project is about and the questions
  that need to be answered.
* [Project Workbook](https://www.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pdfiles/execs/projworkbookJan2016.pdf): This is your handy, must-not-miss, indispensable guide to analyzing your company.
* [Number
  Checking](https://www.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pc/numbercheckerJuly2015.xls): You can check to see whether you are hopelessly
  off on your numbers (and why) by going to this spreadsheet.
* [FAQ](cfprojectsumm.htm): These are some of the issues that have turned up in prior
  iterations. Check to see if your question is answered here.
* [Sample Project Write-Up](Seminars/disneySh.htm): My
  project write up for Disney. Use it as a rough guide but not
  as the only or even the best template for your own write up.
