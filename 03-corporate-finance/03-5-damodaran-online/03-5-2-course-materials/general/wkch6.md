Weekly Challenge 6

used the current market price to estimate the weights for the cost of capital
calculation and then used the cost of capital to come up with an estimate value
of equity (which is different from the market value used in step 1).

would you eliminate the inconsistency from the valuation? In other words, how
would you ensure that you have the same equity value in the cost of capital
calculation and as the ending value for equity?
