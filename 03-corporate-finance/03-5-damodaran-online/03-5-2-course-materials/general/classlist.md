#

![Class List](Budimage/classlist2.png)

In my day job, I teach the Corporate Finance and Valuation classes in the
MBA program at the Stern School of Business. These are semester-long
classes that last 14 weeks and 26 sessions. The classes are webcast
and you can download the lecture notes and other material for the
class. I also teach shorter versions of these classes to the executive
program, which also have webcasts available on them. In the last couple of years, I have also created webcasts specifically for online classes and/or tied to my books. You can
click on the class to get a richer menu of what is available under
each class. I do teach seminars on a range of topics and you can
find a listing of topics with more information by [clicking
here](country.htm).
