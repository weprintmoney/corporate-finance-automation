# Live Meetups

Cleaned transcripts of the six optional live Zoom meetups with Prof. Damodaran (Fall 2026). These are distinct from the 36 asynchronous lesson transcripts in `03-2-course-content/` — meetups are unscripted Q&A and cover material across module boundaries.

Meetup dates and nominal lesson coverage: [`../03-1-course-overview/course-schedule.md`](../03-1-course-overview/course-schedule.md).

## Transcript conventions

Meetup transcripts are dialogue, not prose, because the sessions are Q&A. Three speaker labels are used:

- **Damodaran** — the instructor
- **Charlcye** — the maintainer (source recording captured her audio on a separate channel)
- **Student** — any other participant; **inferred from context**, not identified by name unless the instructor names them aloud

The source transcripts come from automatic speech recognition, which labels every voice other than Charlcye's as one speaker. Splitting those turns is an editorial judgment, so treat `Student:` attribution as approximate.

Garbled proper nouns and factual slips in the source are marked inline with `[ed. — ...]`. Section headings are editorial, added for navigation. Substance is not added or removed.
