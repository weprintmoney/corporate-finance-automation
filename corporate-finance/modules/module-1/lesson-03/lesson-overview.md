# Lesson 3 — The Objective: Reality and Reaction

## Overview

When conflicts of interest abound, markets are inefficient and social costs are part of the equation, maximizing stock prices without constraints can create significant and perhaps even catastrophic costs. This lesson looks at alternative objectives as well as constrained stock price maximization. Students will evaluate corporate governance mechanisms that have developed as alternatives to stock price maximization, examining their strengths and weaknesses, as well as constraints that can be imposed on the different claim holders to minimize the costs of staying stock price focused.

**Learning objectives:**
- Evaluate corporate governance mechanisms developed as alternatives to stock price maximization
- Assess the strengths and weaknesses of alternative objectives
- Analyze constraints that can be imposed on different claim holders to minimize the costs of a stock price focus
