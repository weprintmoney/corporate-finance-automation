# Lesson 12 — Debt: Measure and Cost

## Overview

A firm can not only raise equity but can borrow money. To bring debt into the hurdle rate, we need to be careful about what we consider debt and attach a cost that captures what the firm will need to make over the long term to justify borrowing, as well as the tax benefits of debt. This lesson defines debt (at least for cost of capital or hurdle rate purposes) and develops ways of estimating its cost across different types of companies.

**Learning objectives:**
- Define debt broadly for cost of capital purposes, including operating leases and other debt-like obligations
- Estimate the cost of debt for companies with and without credit ratings
- Calculate the after-tax cost of debt to account for the tax shield on interest payments
