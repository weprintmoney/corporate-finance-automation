# Lesson 2 — The Objective: Utopia and Let Down

## Overview

Conventional corporate finance is built on a singular objective, maximizing stock price. In this lesson, we look at the assumptions you need to make that make this focus possible and follow up by looking at what can go wrong. Students will examine the different interests that underlie the modern publicly-traded company, from stockholders to lenders to managers to markets and society, and what we have to assume for them to all buy into the "stock price maximization" objective, and then look at the potential conflicts between different interests and how they can play out in dysfunctional decisions.

**Learning objectives:**
- Examine the different interests that underlie the modern publicly-traded company: stockholders, lenders, managers, markets, and society
- Identify the assumptions required for all parties to accept the "stock price maximization" objective
- Analyze potential conflicts between different interests and how they can lead to dysfunctional decisions
