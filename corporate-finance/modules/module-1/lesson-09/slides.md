DETERMINANT 2: OPERATING LEVERAGE EFFECTS

• Operating leverage refers to the proportion of the total costs

of the firm that are fixed.

• Other things remaining equal, higher operating leverage

results in greater earnings variability which in turn results in
higher betas.

Aswath Damodaran

8

MEASURES OF OPERATING LEVERAGE

• Fixed Costs Measure = Fixed Costs / Variable Costs

o This measures the relationship between fixed and variable costs. The higher

the proportion, the higher the operating leverage.

• EBIT Variability Measure = % Change in EBIT / % Change in Revenues
o This measures how quickly the earnings before interest and taxes changes
as revenue changes. The higher this number, the greater the operating
leverage.

EFFECTS OF LEVERAGE ON BETAS: DISNEY

• The regression beta for Disney is 1.25. This beta is a

levered beta (because it is based on stock prices, which
reflect leverage) and the leverage implicit in the beta
estimate is the average market debt equity ratio during the
period of the regression (2008 to 2013)

• The average debt equity ratio during this period was

19.44%.

• The unlevered beta for Disney can then be estimated (using

a marginal tax rate of 36.1%)
o = Current Beta / (1 + (1 – tax rate) (Average Debt/Equity))
o = 1.25 / (1 + (1 – 0.361)(0.1944)) = 1.1119
