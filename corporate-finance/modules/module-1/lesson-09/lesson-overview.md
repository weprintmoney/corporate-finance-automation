# Lesson 9 — Beta Fundamentals

## Overview

While we may estimate betas from regressions, betas are determined by choices that companies make — what businesses to be in, how those businesses are run, and how much debt is raised to finance them. This lesson examines the fundamental drivers of beta and how financial leverage affects it, providing a framework for understanding why betas differ across companies and industries.

**Learning objectives:**
- Identify the three fundamental determinants of beta: business type, operating leverage, and financial leverage
- Understand how debt amplifies equity risk and adjust betas for financial leverage (levered vs. unlevered beta)
- Use beta fundamentals to anticipate how a company's beta will change when its business mix or capital structure changes
