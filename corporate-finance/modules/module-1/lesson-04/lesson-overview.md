# Lesson 4 — Define and Measure Risk

## Overview

Risk is a feature of business, not a bug. The objective in business cannot be to avoid all risk but to seek the right risks and exploit them to create value. Risk is a mix of danger and opportunity, and coming up with an effective measure of risk is central to running any business well. This lesson defines risk broadly, specifies the ingredients that would characterize an effective risk measure, and looks at alternative models that have developed in finance to measure risk — from the CAPM to its variants and alternatives (the arbitrage pricing model, multifactor and proxy models).

**Learning objectives:**
- Define risk broadly and specify the ingredients of an effective risk measure
- Evaluate the Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM) and its underlying assumptions
- Compare alternative risk models: APM, multifactor models, and proxy models
