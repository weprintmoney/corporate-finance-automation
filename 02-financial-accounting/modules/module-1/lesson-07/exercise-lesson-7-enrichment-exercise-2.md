## ESSENTIALS OF FINANCIAL ACCOUNTING

## **Enrichment Exercise # 2**

Take a look at the Disney Balance Sheet:

- Compare the Current Ratio for Disney the 3 years 2016 and 2015. Was there a change and what do you think of the change?
- Explanation: https://www.stock-analysis-on.net/NYSE/Company/Walt-Disney-Co/Ratios/Liquidity

Current Ratio = Current Assets / Current Liabilities

2016 Current Ratio: 16,966 / 16,842 = 1.007362546

2015 Current Ratio: 16,758 / 16,334 = 1.0259581242

The amount they owe increased more than the amount they own in assets in 2016.
