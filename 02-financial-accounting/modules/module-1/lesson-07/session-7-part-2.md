And this expense appears on the income statement. Well, in order to calculate these components, we use the concepts of time value of money and discounting future cash flows. The time value of money concept is based on the premise that money grows over time as it earns interest. So think of money moving through time. As we move forward to the future, it grows as it earns interest. And as we move backward in time. It shrinks as we discount the interest or strip it away to calculate the present value. Traditional bonds or notes represent legal agreements, and they often include two components – a promise to pay a specified interest payment at specific times and a promise to repay the principal, which is that amount originally borrowed.

Now, you may hear terms like non-interest-bearing bonds, also called zero-coupon bonds, but this simply means that the borrower will wait and pay all of the interest at maturity when they pay back the principal.
