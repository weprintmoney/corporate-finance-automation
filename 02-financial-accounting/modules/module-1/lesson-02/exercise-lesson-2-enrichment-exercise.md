## **Lesson 2**

## **Enrichment Exercise**

Looking at the Facebook, Inc. Balance Sheet:

- 1. Liabilities + Equity = Total Assets

Total Current Assets are \$34,401 million while Total Assets and Total Liabilities as well as Stockholders' Equity are \$64,961 million. See the FB Balance Sheet.

- 2. Their current assets for the most recent year-end are \$34,401 million.
- 3. There was a significant change compared to the prior year. Last year they had current assets of \$21,652 million so it increased by \$12,749 million or \$12,749 million/\$21,652 million, which is 59%. It appears that their marketable securities had a significant increase from \$13,527 million to \$20,546 million. There were actually many footnote disclosures related to this account throughout. See the FB Footnote Disclosures.
- 4. Facebook's most liquid asset (after cash) are their marketable securities.
- 5. Facebook's liability due the soonest are their accounts payable, which is the first liability they listed in order due.
- 6. Facebook only has a current liabilities of \$2,875 million as compared to current assets of

\$34,401 million, meaning there is no concern as to whether they can pay their bills or not.
