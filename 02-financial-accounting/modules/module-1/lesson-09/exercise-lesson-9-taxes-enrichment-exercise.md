The Yahoo footnote disclosure explains the calculations: The current portion of 98,509 is the amount they will actually pay the government and this is based on the tax reporting rules; the deferred portion represents the difference b/w the rules and this will be paid in the future. As you can see at the bottom, the total provision (benefit) for taxes of 126,228 matches the amount show on the Income Statement
